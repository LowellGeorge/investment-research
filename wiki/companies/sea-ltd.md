# Sea Ltd

> A founder-led Southeast Asian platform company operating the region's dominant e-commerce marketplace (Shopee), a cash-generative gaming franchise (Garena/Free Fire), and a fast-scaling fintech lending business (Monee), generating $23B in revenue at 36% growth with sub-2% capital intensity.

## Business Overview

Sea Limited was founded as Garena Interactive Holding in May 2009 by Forrest Li, a Stanford MBA graduate who believed Southeast Asia's 600 million consumers were dramatically underserved by technology. The company began as a game publisher licensing PC titles across the region, then expanded into digital payments (AirPay, 2014) and e-commerce (Shopee, June 2015). It rebranded to Sea Limited in 2017 and listed on the NYSE in October 2017.

Three structurally different businesses operate under one roof. Shopee is the largest e-commerce platform in Southeast Asia with roughly 52% of regional GMV, 400 million active buyers, and 20 million sellers. FY2025 GMV reached $127 billion at a 12.4% GAAP take rate. Garena operates Free Fire, an eight-year-old mobile battle royale with 100 million daily active users that grew bookings 37% to $2.9 billion. Monee (formerly SeaMoney) is a consumer lending and payments platform with 37 million active credit users, a $9.2 billion loan book, and NPLs at 1.1%.

The company underwent a dramatic pivot in June 2022 from growth-at-all-costs to profitability after the stock collapsed 90%. International expansions into Europe and India were abandoned; ~7,000 employees were cut. The recovery has been striking: FY2025 net income reached $1.6 billion with operating cash flow of $5.0 billion.

## Bull Case

- Shopee's monetization runway is the core thesis: EBITDA/GMV at 0.7% versus 2-3% long-term target; ad take rate at ~2% versus 4-5% target
- Sub-2% capex intensity with $5B+ operating cash flow -- rare for a 36% grower at this scale
- Fortress balance sheet: $10.57B in cash against ~$2.34B convertible notes
- Free Fire's defiance of gaming mortality curves (37% bookings growth in year eight) generates $1.7B in segment EBITDA
- At ~11x EV/EBITDA for 25-35% growth with margin expansion ahead, the market is not giving full credit for monetization runway

## Bear Case

- Forrest Li controls ~57.7% of voting power through dual-class shares while owning only ~16.1% economically -- governance is a benevolent dictatorship
- Monee's $9.2B loan book grew ~80% YoY with a shift from "whitelist-based" to "all-can-apply" lending -- NPLs are mechanically suppressed by rapid denominator growth
- Single-title gaming risk: Free Fire's India ban in February 2022 wiped $16B of market cap in one session
- The 2020-2022 expansion-and-retreat cycle destroyed shareholder value and revealed willingness to pursue aggressive expansion without advance warning
- $1B share buyback authorization but only $14.5M deployed in Q4 2025 -- reads more as checkbox than commitment

## Competitive Position

In Southeast Asian e-commerce, the industry has consolidated into a three-platform oligopoly: Shopee (~52% GMV), TikTok Shop (~18%), and Lazada (~15%). Shopee's competitive moat is logistics: SPX Express processes over 30 million parcels per day with a 6% cost-per-order reduction achieved in 2025. TikTok Shop represents the most credible disruptive threat through social commerce but has lower average order values and undeveloped logistics. In Brazil, MercadoLibre dominates with ~32% market share, though Shopee became market leader by order volume in Q2 2025.

The overarching structural advantage is Sea's flywheel: Shopee generates user data that feeds Monee's credit underwriting, while Garena's EBITDA funds competitive investments across the ecosystem. [[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]] (through Lazada), [[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]] (40% early investor), and [[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]] are the broader competitive reference points.

## Management Assessment

Forrest Li has led the company for its entire seventeen-year existence. The inner circle is unusually tenured: co-founder Gang Ye (COO since 2010), CFO Tony Hou (since 2013), and Chris Feng (Group President, built Shopee from scratch). Management beat guidance on every metric in 2025 -- Shopee GMV guided ~20%, delivered 27%; Garena guided double-digit, delivered 37%. The critical concern is Li's 57.7% voting control with Cayman Islands incorporation providing no meaningful shareholder protections, and his recent election as president of the Football Association of Singapore raising distraction questions.

## Red Flags

- The rapid loan book growth mechanically suppresses NPL ratios -- vintage-level delinquency data is not disclosed
- Cayman Islands incorporation affords shareholders no appraisal rights, derivative suit framework, or proxy statement obligation
- $625-716M annual stock-based compensation regularly adjusted out of "adjusted EBITDA"
- Compensation disclosure is deliberately limited by foreign private issuer status -- no individual executive pay tables
- Management's "all-can-apply" lending shift (Q4 2025) represents a material increase in risk appetite that headline NPLs do not yet capture
