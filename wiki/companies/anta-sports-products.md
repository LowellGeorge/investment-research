# Anta Sports Products

> China's largest sportswear group by market share, operating a multi-brand platform (ANTA, FILA, Descente, Kolon Sport) with a proven ability to acquire underperforming international brands and revitalize them for the Chinese consumer, while holding strategic stakes in Amer Sports (Arc'teryx, Salomon) and Puma.

## Business Overview

ANTA Sports traces its origins to shoe-making workshops in Jinjiang, Fujian Province. Founder Ding Shizhong made his first transformative bet in 1999, staking the company's entire annual profit on a CCTV advertising campaign featuring table tennis champion Kong Linghui. When Kong won Olympic gold in 2000, ANTA became a nationally recognized brand overnight.

The most consequential strategic move came in 2009, when ANTA acquired the FILA trademark for mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macau for approximately RMB 325 million. FILA was then loss-making; it grew to over RMB 28 billion in annual revenue by FY2025 at gross margins above 70%. This brand incubation capability -- acquiring underperforming international IP and revitalizing it with operational discipline -- became ANTA's defining competitive advantage. The 2019 consortium acquisition of Amer Sports (EUR 4.6B, bringing Arc'teryx, Salomon, and Wilson) and the January 2026 acquisition of a 29% stake in Puma (EUR 1.5B) extended this model globally.

ANTA completed a DTC transformation in 2020, bringing over 80% of ANTA-brand stores under direct or controlled operations. The resulting group-level gross margin reached a record 62.6% in 2024. With approximately 21-23% of China's sportswear market, ANTA has displaced Nike (~20.7%) as the country's market share leader, while domestic brands' share of the broader apparel market rose from 35.8% in 2020 to 56.1% by 2024.

## Bull Case

- Proven multi-brand platform with FILA as proof of concept: acquired for RMB 325M, now generating RMB 28.5B revenue at 70%+ gross margins
- 54.5% family ownership provides exceptional alignment; Ding family stake worth approximately USD 14-15 billion
- "Other brands" segment (Descente, Kolon Sport) grew 59.2% in FY2025 to CNY 19B, representing the next wave of growth; Amer Sports stake appreciating in value post-NYSE listing

## Bear Case

- ANTA core brand showing maturation: FY2025 revenue grew only 3.7% with low single-digit 2026 guidance
- Overseas expansion is nascent (CNY 850M, barely 1% of total) with a thin track record of Chinese consumer brands succeeding internationally
- FILA growth has decelerated after reaching scale; three of six executive directors share family ties, creating structural governance weakness

## Competitive Position

China's sportswear market is an oligopoly tightening at the top, valued at over CNY 500 billion and growing above 6% annually. ANTA commands 21-23% market share, followed by Nike at 20.7%, Li Ning at 9.4%, and adidas at 8.7%. The structural displacement of Western incumbents by domestic operators has been dramatic: the share held by Chinese domestic brands rose from 35.8% in 2020 to 56.1% by 2024.

ANTA's moat is a bundle of reinforcing advantages: multi-brand portfolio as an intangible asset (proven brand incubation capability no competitor has replicated), DTC retail providing real-time sell-through data and pricing discipline, scale advantages in R&D (CNY 2.2B invested in FY2025), and growing switching costs through membership programs and children's sportswear. No other company globally, including Nike, spans as many price tiers and category verticals through a single corporate structure.

## Management Assessment

The Ding family controls approximately 54.5% through a one-share-one-vote structure, representing genuine economic exposure worth USD 14-15 billion. The 2023 Co-CEO transition was executed cleanly, splitting mass market (Lai) versus premium/international (Wu). Across multiple earnings calls, guidance has been consistently exceeded. The 50% dividend payout ratio has been honored every year, and an HKD 10 billion buyback program is underway. Management communication is data-rich and operationally specific. Capital allocation has been disciplined with the notable exception of the ambitious Puma stake, which extends the "acquire, restructure, scale" playbook to a global stage.

## Financial Profile

- **Gross Margin:** 62.6% (record, FY2024); driven by DTC transformation and premium brand mix
- **Operating Margin:** 23-24%
- **FCF:** CNY 16.1B (FY2025)
- **Balance Sheet:** Net cash CNY 31.7B
- **ROE:** Strong, driven by genuine margin expansion rather than leverage
- **R&D:** CNY 2.2B (FY2025, +10% YoY)

## Red Flags

- ANTA core brand approaching saturation in China's largest cities with only 3.7% revenue growth
- The Puma stake (EUR 1.5B) is a bold bet without operational control; turnaround requires influencing a European company's strategy from a minority position
- Board composition is executive-heavy (6 executive vs. 4 independent directors) with three family members among executives
- Chinese sportswear market becoming more fragmented as vertical/niche brands grow faster than broad market
- Jack Wolfskin acquisition (USD 290M, May 2025) adds another brand to an already complex multi-brand portfolio
