# FPT Corp

> Vietnam's largest technology company and a rising global IT services challenger, with 55,000+ employees across 28 countries, anchored by a dominant Japan franchise (44% of IT services revenue) and aggressively positioning for the AI transition.

## Business Overview

FPT Corporation traces its origins to September 1988, when Dr. Truong Gia Binh and 12 Soviet-trained scientists established the "Food Processing Technology Company" -- a name that was aspirational camouflage for a technology venture in the final years of central planning. Over 37 years, FPT has transformed from a hardware distributor into Vietnam's dominant technology conglomerate, operating across five segments: Global IT Services (50% of revenue), Telecom (27%), Domestic IT (13%), Education, and Online Advertising.

Global IT Services is the strategic growth engine, generating VND 35.4T in FY2025 revenue (+14.3% YoY). Japan is the franchise -- contributing 44% of global IT services revenue, growing 25.4%, with 17 offices and 20 years of accumulated relationships. FPT has reached the scale of winning nine-figure contracts: a record USD 256M deal with an Asian energy conglomerate and a USD 100M US contract for cloud migration and AI integration. The company has invested aggressively in AI infrastructure, partnering with NVIDIA to establish two AI Factory facilities ranking 36th and 38th among the world's Top 500 supercomputers.

FPT's conglomerate structure includes FPT Telecom (broadband/pay-TV with recurring subscription economics), FPT University (one of Vietnam's top private universities serving as a proprietary talent pipeline), and more recently, English Premier League broadcasting rights and a Chelsea FC sponsorship.

## Bull Case

- Japan franchise is an extraordinarily durable intangible asset: 20 years of relationships, 17 offices, 25.4% growth, and a cultural alignment that competitors cannot replicate quickly
- Large-deal momentum accelerating: 26 deals over USD 10M in FY2025 (up 117% YoY), demonstrating transition from body-shop outsourcer to enterprise transformation partner
- AI-first delivery model (FleziPT ecosystem, NVIDIA partnership, AI Factory infrastructure) positions FPT to ride the AI wave rather than be displaced by it
- 40-50% cost advantage versus Indian IT peers provides structural pricing wedge in global markets

## Bear Case

- AI is existential in both directions: if AI reduces demand for offshore labor by 20-30%, FPT's core business model faces structural headwinds
- Minority interest leakage: in FY2024 only 26% of consolidated net income reached parent shareholders; improved to ~83% in FY2025 but the structure remains complex
- Cash flow deterioration: H1 2025 OCF dropped 46% YoY while short-term borrowings surged 40%, pushing D/E above 1.0x
- Conglomerate sprawl into EPL broadcasting rights, Chelsea FC sponsorship, and education dilutes strategic focus

## Competitive Position

FPT occupies a distinctive niche in global IT services: too large to dismiss as a niche Vietnamese outsourcer, yet still one-twentieth the size of TCS. The company does not compete head-to-head with Indian majors across every vertical; it wins in specific pockets where its cost advantage (Vietnamese developers at USD 15-25/hour versus USD 30-50 in India) and deep domain expertise create defensible positions.

Within Vietnam, FPT faces no peer of comparable breadth. Viettel Solutions and CMC Global are the nearest domestic competitors but diverge materially in strategy. FPT's 28.4% domestic IT market share and 50%+ of revenue from international operations give it a unique profile. The company's moat rests on cost advantage, deepening switching costs through multi-year transformation contracts, and intangible assets accumulated over decades in Japan's relationship-driven enterprise market.

The key competitive question is whether [[wiki/companies/hcl-technologies|HCL Technologies]], TCS, and Infosys, armed with vastly greater scale and deepening AI capabilities, prevent FPT from breaking into the top tier of global IT services.

## Management Assessment

Founder Truong Gia Binh (age 69) remains Executive Chairman with approximately 7.1% ownership, the largest individual stake. He has led FPT since 1988 -- nearly four decades of continuity. The founder-led structure brings vision alignment but concentrates decision-making risk. Binh's charismatic, mission-driven leadership style blends corporate ambition with Vietnamese patriotic narrative. CEO Nguyen Van Khoa handles operational execution. The lack of a clear succession plan for the 69-year-old chairman is the most significant governance risk.

## Red Flags

- Minority interest structure dramatically overstates shareholder economics; only ~83% of FY2025 profit reached parent shareholders (26% in FY2024)
- Short-term borrowings surged to VND 20.3T by H1 2025, creating refinancing risk in a country where credit availability is tied to state banking policy
- Systematic stock dividends (190M shares in 2024, 222M in 2025) dilute EPS while preserving cash -- a stealth mechanism rather than genuine shareholder return
- US revenue declined 0.8% in FY2025, buried in geographic charts and never discussed by management
- Conglomerate empire-building: EPL broadcasting rights through 2031, Chelsea FC sponsorship, goodwill rising to VND 909B
