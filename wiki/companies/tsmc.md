# TSMC

> The world's dominant semiconductor foundry, manufacturing approximately 90% of the most advanced chips on earth, with a 71% foundry market share, 60% gross margins, and a $98B net cash position -- the single most important company in the global technology supply chain.

## Business Overview

TSMC was founded on February 21, 1987, in Hsinchu Science Park by Morris Chang, who was recruited by the Taiwanese government to build a semiconductor industry. Chang's radical insight -- that manufacturing excellence and design innovation were becoming too complex and capital-intensive to coexist within a single firm -- created the "pure-play foundry" model. By never designing its own chips, TSMC eliminated the trust problem that plagued IDMs competing with their own foundry customers. Trust and manufacturing focus became the twin pillars of the competitive moat from day one.

FY2025 revenue reached NT$3.81 trillion (~$91B), up 35.9% YoY. By platform: High-Performance Computing accounted for 58% (+48% YoY), Smartphones 29% (+15% YoY), with IoT, Automotive, and DCE comprising the remainder. Advanced nodes (7nm and below) generated 74% of full-year revenue (77% in Q4). North America represented 75-77% of revenue. The AI explosion has made TSMC's most advanced nodes and CoWoS advanced packaging the bottleneck for the entire AI industry.

Every major AI accelerator -- NVIDIA's Blackwell, AMD's Instinct, Google's TPUs, Amazon's Trainium -- depends on TSMC. The company has committed $165B+ to US manufacturing (Arizona fabs operational), with additional fabs in Japan (Kumamoto) and Germany (Dresden). CEO C.C. Wei succeeded Morris Chang in 2018 and has overseen revenue growth from ~$34B to over $90B.

## Bull Case

- Natural monopoly at the bleeding edge: 71% foundry market share, 90%+ of advanced node production, no credible challenger within 3-5 years
- AI accelerator revenue CAGR upgraded to "mid-to-high 50s percent" -- demand consistently exceeding expectations
- ROE of 35.4%, ROIC of ~54%, gross margins at 59.9% (Q4 at 62.3%) -- financial characteristics matched by very few companies at any scale
- NT$3.07T (~$98B) in cash with fortress balance sheet; free cash flow of NT$1T ($31B) despite $40.9B in capex
- Management credibility is exceptional: guidance beaten for four consecutive quarters, targets raised three times in eighteen months

## Bear Case

- 90% of advanced capacity remains in Taiwan, 100 miles from mainland China -- investors pricing near-zero probability of disruption into a $1.47T market cap
- FY2026 capex of $52-56B is the largest single-year semiconductor investment in history; CEO Wei admitted "I'm also very nervous about it"
- Customer concentration shifting and intensifying: NVIDIA overtook Apple as largest customer (~22% of revenue, ~$33B)
- Overseas fabs cost 5-20% more per chip; long-term gross margin floor quietly shifted to "56% and higher" from the current 60%
- 25% revenue CAGR target through 2029 relies entirely on AI demand sustaining a mid-to-high 50s CAGR -- history is littered with infrastructure spending busts

## Competitive Position

The foundry industry is a natural oligopoly where TSMC's dominance is unprecedented and widening. [[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]] Foundry holds a distant ~8-9% share with persistent yield challenges. Intel Foundry Services remains sub-5% and lost $13B in FY2024. TSMC's N2 (2nm) entered volume production in 2025 while competitors struggle to match the prior generation.

Four reinforcing moats define the position. Technology leadership: 77% of Q4 wafer revenue from 7nm and below. Switching costs: designing a chip for a specific foundry's process is a multi-year, multi-hundred-million-dollar undertaking, with TSMC's 16,000+ design enablement IPs creating deep lock-in. Efficient scale: higher volumes fund greater R&D and capex, producing better yields that attract more customers. Advanced packaging monopoly: CoWoS capacity is being scaled from 35,000 to 130,000 wafers/month but demand still outstrips supply.

TSMC's pricing power is evident in successive 10-20% price increases for 2nm wafers -- yet customers continue to queue for capacity. [[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]], [[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]], [[wiki/companies/mediatek|Mediatek]], and [[wiki/companies/quanta-computer|Quanta Computer]] are all part of the broader semiconductor ecosystem in which TSMC sits as the central node.

## Management Assessment

CEO/Chairman C.C. Wei holds a PhD from Yale and has been at TSMC for 27+ years, named to TIME's 100 Most Influential People in AI. CFO Wendell Huang delivers precise forward guidance with narrow ranges. The board has seven independent directors (70%), including former CEOs of BT Group, Applied Materials, Xilinx, Xerox, and Sunoco, plus former MIT President. Wei's total compensation is ~$29M, 98.3% performance-linked. Succession planning is underway with Co-COOs Y.P. Chyn and Dr. Y.J. Mii elevated in 2024. Management consistently under-promised and over-delivered: actual FY2025 growth of 35.9% versus initial "mid-20s percent" guidance.

## Financial Profile

- **Operating Margin:** 50.8% (FY2025)
- **ROE:** 35.4%; ROIC ~54%
- **Operating Cash Flow:** NT$2.28T (132% of net income)
- **Free Cash Flow:** NT$1.003T ($31B)
- **Cash:** NT$3.07T (~$98B); net cash ~NT$2.04T (~$68B)
- **Capex:** NT$1.27T ($40.9B, FY2025); $52-56B guided FY2026
- **Valuation:** ~23x forward P/E; PEG below 1.0

## Red Flags

- Geographic concentration: 80%+ of leading-edge capacity in Taiwan despite $165B US commitment -- meaningful derisking not before 2030
- Arizona culture clashes: lawsuit by 28 employees alleging "anti-American culture," communication predominantly in Mandarin
- Customer rotation from predictable consumer (Apple) to more volatile enterprise AI (NVIDIA) increases revenue cyclicality
- N2 ramp adds 2-3% margin dilution in 2026 on top of 3-4% overseas fab dilution
- New-hire turnover reached 17.6% -- a five-year high; Glassdoor work-life balance scores 2.3/5 -- TSMC extracts extraordinary productivity at significant human cost
