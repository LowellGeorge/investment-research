# China Mengniu Dairy

> China's second-largest dairy company and clear #1 in cheese and chilled dairy, transitioning from an acquisitive growth model to a cash-generative, margin-focused business during the worst industry downturn since 2008 -- a decent franchise trapped in a difficult industry with mediocre governance.

## Business Overview

China Mengniu Dairy was founded in August 1999 by Niu Gensheng, a former Yili Group vice president who was dismissed following internal power struggles. Niu and 50+ former Yili subordinates pooled resources to establish a direct competitor, naming it "Mengniu" (combining "Mongolia" and "cow/ox"). The company grew at a compound annual rate of roughly 158% over its first seven years, surpassing Yili by 2007 to become China's largest dairy company.

The 2008 melamine scandal was a near-death experience: 10% of Mengniu's liquid milk tested positive for contamination. In 2009, state-owned COFCO Group acquired a 20% stake for HK$6.1 billion, effectively nationalizing the company and ending the founder-led era permanently. Under COFCO's stewardship, Mengniu pursued aggressive M&A -- Yashili (HK$12.4B, 2013), Modern Dairy, Bellamy's Organic (A$1.5B, 2019), and Milkground (cheese) -- a campaign that has been largely value-destructive, generating billions in impairments.

Revenue has fallen 17% from its FY2023 peak of RMB 98.6 billion, with liquid milk (79% of revenue) declining from RMB 82.1 billion to RMB 64.9 billion. However, the cash generation story is genuinely impressive: record OCF of RMB 8.75 billion and FCF of RMB 6.3 billion in FY2025 during a year of revenue decline, with a negative cash conversion cycle (-12.7 days) that means growth would actually release working capital.

## Bull Case

- Record FCF of RMB 6.3B at an ~8% FCF yield on current market cap; negative cash conversion cycle is a structural advantage
- Category leaders in cheese (#1, ~49.5% share, growing 22%), chilled dairy (leader for 21 consecutive years), and premium fresh milk
- Gross margin expanded 460bps over three years to record 39.9%; CapEx slashed 38% cumulatively as company enters harvest mode

## Bear Case

- Revenue decline is structural, not cyclical: liquid milk fell 21% in two years; the old RMB 150B revenue target has been quietly abandoned
- Value-destructive M&A track record: Bellamy's (RMB 4.6B impairment), Modern Dairy (RMB 910M biological asset impairments in H1 2025), Yashili NZ disposed after years of losses
- COFCO governance creates permanent alignment gap: CEO holds ~0.011%; chairman is a COFCO deputy GM; management careers depend on COFCO's internal evaluation, not stock price

## Competitive Position

China's dairy industry is a ~USD 75 billion market structured as a concentrated duopoly. Yili leads with approximately 24% national market share versus Mengniu's 18%, and the gap is not closing. Yili's revenue likely exceeds RMB 120 billion, dwarfing Mengniu's RMB 82 billion. Below the duopoly sit Bright Dairy, China Feihe, Junlebao, and New Hope Dairy, none exceeding mid-single-digit share.

Mengniu holds clear category leads in chilled dairy (21 consecutive years), retail cheese (Milkground at ~49.5% share), and premium fresh milk (Shiny Meadow #1 across hypermarkets and e-commerce). The distribution network reaches millions of retail terminals, and manufacturing efficiency includes the world's first "Lighthouse" certified liquid milk plant. The duopoly benefits from substantial structural moats: cold-chain requirements, regulatory compliance costs, and brand trust. However, competitive intensity between Mengniu and Yili is fierce, with selling expenses consuming 26.3% of revenue.

## Management Assessment

CEO Gao Fei is a 25-year Mengniu veteran with a heavily sales-weighted background. COFCO's grip is tightening: the chairman is a COFCO deputy GM, the new CFO is a COFCO veteran, and the former CEO's departure has the hallmarks of a managed exit. Insider ownership is negligible -- CEO Gao Fei holds approximately 0.011% of the company. Recent capital allocation has been markedly better than the historical M&A record: CapEx cuts delivered, rising dividends materialized, and operating margins expanded as promised. The fundamental governance risk is that food security objectives or political alignment may override purely commercial decisions -- the reluctance to exit loss-making Modern Dairy upstream is the clearest example.

## Financial Profile

- **Gross Margin:** 39.9% (record, FY2025; up 460bps over three years)
- **Operating Margin:** 8.0%
- **Selling Expenses:** 26.3% of revenue (fierce competitive intensity)
- **OCF:** RMB 8.75B (record); FCF: RMB 6.3B (record)
- **D/E Ratio:** 53.8% (down from 81.3%)
- **ROE:** ~8-9% adjusted (depressed by impairments; below cost of equity)
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** Negative 12.7 days (structural advantage)

## Red Flags

- Cascading write-downs across three years: RMB 4.33B in FY2024, RMB 2.2-2.4B in FY2025; management frames these as "non-recurring" despite recurring annually
- Effective tax rate spiked to 77.5% (FY2024) then 44.2% (FY2025) -- wildly abnormal for a Chinese consumer staples company
- "Adjusted profit" (RMB 3.96B) diverges significantly from reported net profit (RMB 1.55B), straining credibility
- Related party transactions with COFCO Group Companies are extensive, creating risk that Mengniu functions partly as a captive buyer for COFCO's agricultural output
- HKD 3.6B convertible bond maturing in 2026 adds refinancing risk
- Liquid milk in rapid decline: 21% revenue drop in two years for the dominant segment
