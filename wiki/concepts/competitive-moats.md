# Competitive Moats

Sources of durable competitive advantage identified across the investment research wiki.

## Technology / Process Moats

- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — Multi-generational process technology lead; 71% foundry market share; customer trust covenant (never designs own chips)
- **[[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]]** — HBM technology leadership; first-mover in HBM3E for AI accelerators
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Vertically integrated across memory, logic, displays, and consumer electronics
- **[[wiki/companies/mediatek|MediaTek]]** — Design capability across mobile, IoT, and automotive SoCs

## Network Effects

- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — WeChat's 1.3B+ MAUs creating a social-commerce-payments ecosystem
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** — Largest e-commerce marketplace in China by GMV with embedded logistics
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Social commerce mechanics generating viral, zero-cost user acquisition
- **[[wiki/companies/full-truck-alliance|Full Truck Alliance]]** — Two-sided freight matching marketplace with network density advantages
- **[[wiki/companies/sea-ltd|Sea Ltd]]** — Shopee marketplace + SeaMoney payments creating reinforcing ecosystem

## Distribution Moats

- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — 3,033 owned-property stores in rural/peri-urban Poland; format replication machine
- **[[wiki/companies/pool-corp|POOL Corp]]** — Dominant swimming pool distribution network in North America
- **[[wiki/companies/wal-mart-de-mexico-sab|Walmex]]** — 3,600+ stores across Mexico and Central America
- **[[wiki/companies/capitec-bank|Capitec Bank]]** — Technology-led mass-market banking reaching underserved South African consumers

## Brand Moats

- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** — Multi-brand portfolio (ANTA, FILA, Arc'teryx via Amer Sports) spanning mass to premium
- **[[wiki/companies/techtronic|Techtronic]]** — MILWAUKEE brand dominance in professional power tools
- **[[wiki/companies/nintendo|Nintendo]]** — Mario, Zelda, Pokemon franchises with 40+ years of cultural resonance

## Regulatory Moats

- **[[wiki/companies/aia|AIA]]** — One of few foreign insurers with wholly-owned life insurance license in China
- **[[wiki/companies/computer-age-management-services|CAMS]]** — Dominant mutual fund registrar in India with regulatory barriers to entry
- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]** — Banking license and deposit franchise in a market with limited new licenses

## Scale Moats

- **[[wiki/companies/microsoft|Microsoft]]** — Enterprise software ecosystem (Azure, Office 365, LinkedIn) with massive switching costs
- **[[wiki/companies/midea-group|Midea Group]]** — Largest home appliance manufacturer globally by revenue
- **[[wiki/companies/csl-holdings|CSL]]** — Plasma collection network scale creating biological barriers to entry
