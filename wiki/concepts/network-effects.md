# Network Effects

Platforms and businesses with demand-side scale economies, where each additional user increases the value for all existing users.

## Strong Network Effects

### Two-Sided Marketplaces
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** — Taobao/Tmall connecting hundreds of millions of buyers with millions of merchants; logistics network (Cainiao) reinforces marketplace stickiness
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Social commerce mechanics where group buying creates viral user acquisition at near-zero cost; 70M users in first year
- **[[wiki/companies/sea-ltd|Sea Ltd]]** — Shopee marketplace combining e-commerce with SeaMoney payments; reinforcing loops across Southeast Asia
- **[[wiki/companies/full-truck-alliance|Full Truck Alliance]]** — Freight matching platform connecting truckers with shippers; density of supply improves matching quality

### Social/Communication Platforms
- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — WeChat (1.3B+ MAUs) is effectively China's digital infrastructure — messaging, payments (WeChat Pay), mini-programs, and social commerce all in one ecosystem
- **[[wiki/companies/netease|Netease]]** — Gaming communities with strong user engagement and content creation

### Payment Networks
- **[[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]]** — 100M+ customers creating data flywheel for credit underwriting; near-zero CAC through word-of-mouth
- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — PayLah! as Singapore's most widely used mobile payments platform

## Platform-Adjacent Network Effects

### Developer Ecosystems
- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — 16,000+ design enablement IPs; foundry ecosystem where the tools, libraries, and design rules create deep lock-in for chip designers
- **[[wiki/companies/microsoft|Microsoft]]** — Azure cloud + Office 365 + GitHub + LinkedIn creating interconnected professional/developer ecosystem

### Data Network Effects
- **[[wiki/companies/computer-age-management-services|CAMS]]** — India's dominant mutual fund registrar; more funds → more data → better infrastructure → more funds choose CAMS
- **[[wiki/companies/qualitas-controladora|Qualitas Controladora]]** — Mexico auto insurance data advantage from 30%+ market share improves pricing accuracy

## Weak or Absent Network Effects

- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Physical retail without network dynamics (but strong distribution density moat)
- **[[wiki/companies/csl-holdings|CSL]]** — Biotech moat from R&D and plasma collection scale, not network effects
- **[[wiki/companies/techtronic|Techtronic]]** — Brand and distribution moat without meaningful demand-side scale
