# Regulatory Risk

Government and regulatory exposure varies significantly across the portfolio. This page maps regulatory risk intensity.

## High Regulatory Exposure

### China Technology & Internet
Companies subject to China's evolving regulatory framework:
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** — $2.8B antitrust fine in 2021; restructuring into six business groups; ongoing regulatory adaptation
- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — Game approval freezes, fintech regulation, anti-monopoly scrutiny
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Data security concerns; temporary Play Store suspension over malware allegations
- **[[wiki/companies/netease|Netease]]** — Game license approvals as periodic bottleneck

### Indian Financial Services
Reserve Bank of India oversight:
- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]** — RBI-imposed digital lending restrictions (later lifted); HDFC Ltd merger regulatory complexity
- **[[wiki/companies/icici-bank|ICICI Bank]]** — Banking regulation, PSL requirements, interconnected lending norms
- **[[wiki/companies/kotak-mahindra-bank|Kotak Mahindra Bank]]** — RBI promoter shareholding dilution requirements
- **[[wiki/companies/icici-lombard|ICICI Lombard]]** — IRDAI insurance regulation; motor third-party pricing controls

### Semiconductor Geopolitics
- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — ~90% of world's most advanced chips on an island 100 miles from mainland China; US CHIPS Act compliance; geographic diversification mandates
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Export controls on advanced chip manufacturing equipment
- **[[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]]** — China fab restrictions; Huawei supply chain complications

## Moderate Regulatory Exposure

- **[[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]]** — Brazilian central bank oversight; expansion into Mexico and Colombia brings multi-jurisdiction complexity
- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — MAS regulation (Singapore); navigating multiple Asian banking regimes
- **[[wiki/companies/credicorp|Credicorp]]** — Peruvian political instability affecting banking sector sentiment

## Lower Regulatory Exposure

- **[[wiki/companies/techtronic|Techtronic]]** — Industrial products with limited regulatory overhang
- **[[wiki/companies/pool-corp|POOL Corp]]** — US distribution business with straightforward regulatory environment
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** — Consumer brand with limited regulatory exposure

## VIE Structure Risk

Several Chinese companies use Variable Interest Entity structures that create legal uncertainty:
- [[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]], [[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]], [[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]], [[wiki/companies/trip-com|Trip.com]], [[wiki/companies/full-truck-alliance|Full Truck Alliance]]
