# Cyclicality

Companies exposed to economic and industry cycles. Understanding cyclical dynamics is critical for entry timing and position sizing.

## Highly Cyclical

### Semiconductors
- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — Revenue fell 8.7% in FY2023 before rising 35.9% in FY2025. AI demand is creating a secular floor but does not eliminate cyclicality
- **[[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]]** — Memory prices can swing 50%+ through cycles; HBM provides some insulation but DRAM/NAND remain cyclical
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Memory business drives earnings volatility; counter-cyclical investment strategy exploits downturns
- **[[wiki/companies/silergy|Silergy]]** — Power management IC demand closely tracks end-market capex cycles

### Industrials
- **[[wiki/companies/airtac-international|Airtac International]]** — Pneumatic automation demand tied to Chinese manufacturing capex cycles
- **[[wiki/companies/hongfa-technology|Hongfa Technology]]** — Relay demand correlated with industrial and automotive production cycles
- **[[wiki/companies/quanta-computer|Quanta Computer]]** — Server demand subject to enterprise and hyperscaler investment cycles

## Moderately Cyclical

### Banks
- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]**, **[[wiki/companies/icici-bank|ICICI Bank]]**, **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — Credit cycles affect asset quality; interest rate movements impact margins. However, well-managed banks use downturns to gain market share from weaker competitors
- **[[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]]** — Still proving credit quality through a full cycle; Brazil's macro volatility adds uncertainty

### Consumer Discretionary
- **[[wiki/companies/huazhu-group|Huazhu Group]]** — Hotel RevPAR fluctuates with travel demand and economic cycles
- **[[wiki/companies/trip-com|Trip.com]]** — Travel spend is discretionary and sensitive to consumer confidence
- **[[wiki/companies/sony|Sony]]** — Gaming console cycles (PS5 lifecycle), electronics demand

## Defensive / Low Cyclicality

- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Food retail with 41% fresh food share; consistent demand regardless of macro
- **[[wiki/companies/wal-mart-de-mexico-sab|Walmex]]** — Staples-weighted retail mix provides resilience
- **[[wiki/companies/aia|AIA]]** — Life insurance premiums are sticky; VONB discipline smooths volatility
- **[[wiki/companies/computer-age-management-services|CAMS]]** — India mutual fund infrastructure; growing regardless of market direction
- **[[wiki/companies/resmed|Resmed]]** — Medical devices for chronic conditions (sleep apnea); non-discretionary demand
- **[[wiki/companies/microsoft|Microsoft]]** — Enterprise software with high switching costs; subscription model provides recurring revenue
