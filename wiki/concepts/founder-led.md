# Founder-Led Companies

Companies still led by or heavily influenced by their founders. Founder involvement often correlates with long-term thinking, cultural coherence, and willingness to make contrarian bets — but can also create governance risks around succession and related-party transactions.

## Active Founder-CEOs

- **[[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]]** — David Velez (CEO since founding in 2013). Colombian-born, Stanford-educated, ex-Sequoia partner. Built Nubank from a single credit card product to 100M+ customer Latin American fintech platform
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** — Ding Shizhong (Chairman, founder since 1994). From shoe factory worker's son to builder of China's largest sportswear group. Known for asymmetric bets (FILA acquisition, Amer Sports)
- **[[wiki/companies/fpt-corp|FPT Corp]]** — Truong Gia Binh (Chairman, co-founder 1988). Vietnam's technology pioneer who built FPT from a government research spinoff into the country's largest IT company

## Founder-Influenced (Stepped Back)

- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Tomasz Biernacki (51% owner). Has never sold a share, takes zero compensation, doesn't speak publicly. No CEO since 2020. Company communicates exclusively through written reports. Biernacki's Krot-Invest builds every Dino store without competitive bidding — a permanent related-party tension
- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — Pony Ma (co-founder, still Chairman/CEO). More delegator than hands-on operator; built a culture of internal competition and investment-driven portfolio construction
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Colin Huang resigned as Chairman in March 2021 at age 41, the same day Pinduoduo surpassed Alibaba in active buyers. Supervoting shares entrusted to board. Company operates without a public founder figure

## Founder Legacy Companies

- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — Morris Chang founded TSMC in 1987, retired as chairman in 2018 at age 86. His "pure-play foundry" vision and customer trust covenant remain the company's DNA under C.C. Wei
- **[[wiki/companies/capitec-bank|Capitec Bank]]** — Michiel le Roux founded Capitec in 2001, stepped back from CEO role but remains influential through board and shareholding
- **[[wiki/companies/midea-group|Midea Group]]** — He Xiangjian founded the company in 1968, passed leadership to professional managers. Rare successful Chinese founder-to-professional management transition

## Governance Tensions in Founder-Led Companies

1. **Succession risk** — When does founder step back? Is the bench ready? (See [[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]] as a model for deliberate succession)
2. **Related-party transactions** — [[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]'s Krot-Invest relationship is the most prominent example
3. **Dual-class shares** — Several companies (Alibaba, PDD, Sea Ltd) use supervoting structures that concentrate control
4. **Founder alignment vs. minority rights** — High founder ownership creates alignment but can enable unchecked decision-making
