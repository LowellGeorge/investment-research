# China Risk

Navigating China's regulatory, geopolitical, and governance landscape. With 14 companies in the wiki headquartered in mainland China and several more with significant China exposure, this is a portfolio-level risk factor.

## Companies with Primary China Exposure

### Internet & Technology
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** — $2.8B antitrust fine (2021); restructured into six business groups; ongoing regulatory adaptation required
- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — Game approval freezes, fintech regulation under Ant Financial crackdown spillover, anti-monopoly scrutiny
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Data security concerns; CNN-reported malware allegations led to temporary Google Play Store suspension
- **[[wiki/companies/netease|Netease]]** — Game license approvals as periodic bottleneck; content regulation
- **[[wiki/companies/full-truck-alliance|Full Truck Alliance]]** — Cybersecurity review imposed shortly after US IPO

### Consumer & Industrial
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** — Consumer brand with lower regulatory risk but exposed to Chinese consumption cycles
- **[[wiki/companies/huazhu-group|Huazhu Group]]** — Hospitality sector subject to COVID-related restrictions (historically) and consumer sentiment
- **[[wiki/companies/china-mengniu-dairy|China Mengniu Dairy]]** — Food safety regulation; SOE governance complexity
- **[[wiki/companies/china-resources-beer|China Resources Beer]]** — SOE parentage; premiumization strategy exposed to consumer confidence
- **[[wiki/companies/midea-group|Midea Group]]** — Manufacturing giant with export exposure to trade tensions
- **[[wiki/companies/shenzhen-mindray|Shenzhen Mindray]]** — Medical devices benefiting from domestic substitution push but facing potential export restrictions
- **[[wiki/companies/zhejiang-chint-electrics|Zhejiang Chint Electrics]]** — Electrical equipment; beneficiary of domestic energy infrastructure spending

## Companies with Significant China Revenue

- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — ~10% of revenue from China; restricted from selling advanced chips to sanctioned entities; geopolitical risk from Taiwan Strait
- **[[wiki/companies/aia|AIA]]** — Wholly-owned life insurance license in China is a strategic asset but also creates concentration risk
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Xi'an NAND fab; Chinese consumer electronics revenue

## Risk Dimensions

### Regulatory
- Antitrust enforcement (Common Prosperity campaign)
- Data security and privacy laws (PIPL, DSL)
- Game approvals and content restrictions
- Financial services regulation (fintech crackdown)

### Geopolitical
- US-China technology decoupling
- Taiwan Strait conflict risk (existential for TSMC)
- Sanctions and entity list designations
- VIE structure legal uncertainty

### Governance
- SOE influence in nominally private companies
- Party committee involvement in corporate decision-making
- Related-party transactions common in conglomerate structures
- Limited shareholder rights for foreign minority investors

### Macro
- Property sector deleveraging impact on consumer confidence
- Youth unemployment and consumption weakness
- Local government debt overhang
- Currency management and capital controls
