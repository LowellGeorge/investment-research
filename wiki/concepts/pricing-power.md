# Pricing Power

Ability to raise prices without losing volume — the single most important indicator of a durable business franchise. Companies with true pricing power can maintain or expand margins through inflationary environments.

## Exceptional Pricing Power

- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — Implemented successive price increases (10-20% for 2nm wafers over 3nm); customers queue for capacity regardless. Gross margins expanded from mid-40s to 62.3% over five years. The strongest pricing power in the semiconductor industry
- **[[wiki/companies/microsoft|Microsoft]]** — Enterprise software with high switching costs; regular price increases across Office 365, Azure, and LinkedIn without meaningful churn
- **[[wiki/companies/techtronic|Techtronic]]** — MILWAUKEE brand commands premium pricing in professional power tools; contractors prioritize reliability over cost

## Strong Pricing Power

- **[[wiki/companies/aia|AIA]]** — VONB discipline allows premium pricing on protection products; agency model avoids rate competition
- **[[wiki/companies/csl-holdings|CSL]]** — Plasma-derived therapies with limited supply; biological complexity creates barriers; pricing reflects medical necessity
- **[[wiki/companies/fisher-paykel|Fisher & Paykel]]** — Respiratory care devices in clinical settings where switching is costly and dangerous; specified by clinicians, not procurement
- **[[wiki/companies/resmed|Resmed]]** — Sleep apnea devices with consumable mask replacement cycle; brand loyalty and clinical evidence support pricing

## Moderate Pricing Power

- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]**, **[[wiki/companies/icici-bank|ICICI Bank]]** — Deposit franchises provide funding cost advantages; can maintain NIMs through cycle. But lending rates are competitive
- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — CASA ratio advantage provides structural NIM support
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** — FILA brand has premium pricing power; ANTA core brand competes more on value
- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Proximity format and fresh food focus reduce direct price comparison with discounters; but grocery is inherently competitive

## Limited Pricing Power

- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Memory pricing is commodity-driven by supply-demand balance; consumer electronics face intense competition
- **[[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]]** — HBM has temporary pricing power due to scarcity; standard DRAM/NAND is commodity pricing
- **[[wiki/companies/quanta-computer|Quanta Computer]]** — ODM model with thin margins; pricing set by hyperscaler customers
- **[[wiki/companies/hcl-technologies|HCL Technologies]]** — IT services pricing under pressure from talent cost inflation and competition
