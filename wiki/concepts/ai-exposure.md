# AI Exposure

Companies positioned to benefit from or potentially be disrupted by artificial intelligence.

## Direct Beneficiaries (Infrastructure)

### Semiconductor Manufacturing
- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — The bottleneck for AI. Every major AI accelerator (NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, Amazon Trainium) is fabricated at TSMC. HPC reached 58% of revenue. CoWoS advanced packaging is supply-constrained even at 75,000 wafers/month
- **[[wiki/companies/sk-hynix|SK Hynix]]** — HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) leader. HBM is the critical memory component in AI accelerators. First-mover advantage in HBM3E gives pricing power and supply allocation priority
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Second-largest HBM supplier; DRAM and NAND for data center storage; chasing SK Hynix on HBM yields

### Hardware & Servers
- **[[wiki/companies/quanta-computer|Quanta Computer]]** — Major ODM for AI servers; direct supplier to hyperscalers (Google, Meta, Microsoft). AI server revenue growing rapidly as a share of total
- **[[wiki/companies/mediatek|MediaTek]]** — Edge AI chip development for smartphones and IoT devices; ASIC design partnerships for custom AI chips

### Semiconductor Design Support
- **[[wiki/companies/silergy|Silergy]]** — Power management ICs used in server power delivery; indirect beneficiary of data center buildout

## Software & Platform Beneficiaries

- **[[wiki/companies/microsoft|Microsoft]]** — Azure OpenAI partnership; Copilot integration across Office, GitHub, Windows. Positioned as the enterprise gateway to AI
- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** — Hunyuan LLM; AI integration across gaming, cloud, and enterprise services
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** — Qwen LLM; Alibaba Cloud as China's leading cloud platform positioning for AI workloads

## Potential Disruption Risks

### IT Services
- **[[wiki/companies/hcl-technologies|HCL Technologies]]** — AI-driven code generation could reduce demand for outsourced software development and maintenance
- **[[wiki/companies/fpt-corp|FPT Corp]]** — Similar exposure; partially offset by FPT's own AI capability building

### Search & Content
- **[[wiki/companies/netease|Netease]]** — AI-generated content could supplement or compete with human-created gaming content
- **[[wiki/companies/sea-ltd|Sea Ltd]]** — AI tools enabling smaller sellers could reduce marketplace stickiness

## AI-Neutral Companies

Most banking, insurance, consumer staples, and industrial companies in the wiki have limited direct AI exposure. Their competitive positions are built on distribution, regulation, brand, or physical assets that AI does not directly disrupt:
- [[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]], [[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]], [[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]], [[wiki/companies/pool-corp|POOL Corp]], [[wiki/companies/aia|AIA]]
