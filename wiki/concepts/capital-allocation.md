# Capital Allocation

How management deploys cash is often the single most important determinant of long-term shareholder returns. This page tracks capital allocation patterns across the investment research wiki.

## Exemplary Capital Allocators

- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — Disciplined CapEx guidance consistently met or beaten; transparent dividend policy (TWD 18/share in 2025, TWD 23+ guided for 2026); no buybacks but fortress balance sheet with ~$98B cash
- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — 15-year transformation from regional lender to Asia's most profitable bank; disciplined M&A after learning from expensive Dao Heng acquisition; consistent dividend growth
- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]** — Built India's highest-quality bank through decades of disciplined organic growth and selective acquisitions (Times Bank, HDFC Ltd merger)
- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Self-funding growth flywheel: negative working capital, owned real estate, ROIC 17-22%, net cash balance sheet while opening 345 stores/year
- **[[wiki/companies/capitec-bank|Capitec Bank]]** — Consistently high returns on equity while expanding into new product lines; founder-aligned capital deployment
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports Products]]** — FILA acquisition for RMB 325M became a RMB 20B+ revenue brand; Amer Sports acquisition scaled the multi-brand platform globally

## Concerning Capital Allocation

- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Historical chaebol capital misallocation; improving under shareholder pressure but cross-shareholding structures remain
- **[[wiki/companies/lg-corp|LG Corp]]** — Holding company discount reflecting conglomerate complexity
- **[[wiki/companies/jardine-matheson-holdings|Jardine Matheson]]** — Conglomerate structure with persistent NAV discount

## Self-Funding Growth Models

Several companies operate models where operations fully fund growth without requiring external capital:

- [[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]] — Negative net working capital (-17 days), owned land, vertical meat integration
- [[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]] — Near-zero customer acquisition cost ($5/customer) via organic virality
- [[wiki/companies/pool-corp|POOL Corp]] — Dominant distribution network generates cash for consistent returns

## Key Patterns

1. **Founder alignment matters** — Companies where founders retain significant stakes (Dino Polska 51%, Tencent, TSMC founding culture) tend to show longer-horizon capital allocation
2. **Emerging market banks** that successfully navigate growth vs. asset quality tend to create extraordinary value
3. **Technology companies** with pricing power (TSMC, Microsoft) can fund massive CapEx while maintaining high returns
