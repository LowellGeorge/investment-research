# Emerging Market Premiums

Quality companies in underpenetrated markets command premium valuations because they combine structural growth with proven execution. This page tracks the highest-quality emerging market franchises.

## India — Banking Underpenetration

India's credit-to-GDP ratio remains well below developed market levels, offering a multi-decade growth runway for well-managed banks:

- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]** (~$123B market cap) — India's highest-quality private bank. Built over 30 years of disciplined lending and organic growth. Post-HDFC Ltd merger creates the largest private bank by assets
- **[[wiki/companies/icici-bank|ICICI Bank]]** (~$93B) — India's second-largest private bank. Recovered from the Chanda Kochhar governance crisis to deliver strong asset quality under Sandeep Bakhshi
- **[[wiki/companies/kotak-mahindra-bank|Kotak Mahindra Bank]]** (~$38B) — Premium franchise built by Uday Kotak from a single bill discounting license to a diversified financial services group

## Latin America — Fintech & Financial Inclusion

- **[[wiki/companies/nu-holdings|Nu Holdings]]** (~$66B) — The defining Latin American fintech story. 100M+ customers, expanding from Brazil to Mexico and Colombia. Proving that digital-first banking can achieve profitability at scale in emerging markets
- **[[wiki/companies/credicorp|Credicorp]]** (~$25.6B) — Peru's dominant financial conglomerate. Conservative underwriting culture in a volatile political environment
- **[[wiki/companies/regional-s-a-b|Regional S.A.B.]]** (~$2.7B) — Mexican regional bank with niche focus on SME lending and agricultural finance

## Southeast Asia — Digitizing Economies

- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** (~$126B) — Singapore's largest bank and the regional gold standard for digital transformation
- **[[wiki/companies/pt-bank-central-asia|PT Bank Central Asia]]** (~$48.6B) — Indonesia's most profitable bank. Dominant transactional banking franchise in the world's fourth-largest population
- **[[wiki/companies/bank-of-the-philippine-islands|BPI]]** (~$8.4B) — Philippines' oldest bank with strong deposit franchise

## China — Consumer & Technology

- **[[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]]** (~$567B) — WeChat ecosystem underpinning China's digital economy
- **[[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]]** (~$274B) — China's e-commerce and cloud infrastructure leader, trading at a significant discount to Western peers
- **[[wiki/companies/anta-sports-products|Anta Sports]]** (~$27B) — Multi-brand sportswear platform capturing China's consumer upgrading trend

## Valuation Patterns

1. **Quality banks command 3-5x book** in India and Southeast Asia, reflecting long growth runways
2. **China discount** — Chinese companies trade at compressed multiples vs. quality, reflecting regulatory and geopolitical risk premiums
3. **Latin American fintech premium** — Nu Holdings trades at a significant premium to traditional LatAm banks, priced for continued disruption
