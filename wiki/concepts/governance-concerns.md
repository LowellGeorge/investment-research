# Governance Concerns

Companies with notable governance red flags or structures that warrant monitoring. Governance concerns don't necessarily make a company uninvestable, but they must be priced into the investment thesis.

## Structural Governance Issues

### Controlling Shareholders / Dual-Class Shares
- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — Tomasz Biernacki owns 51%; the Krot-Invest related-party construction arrangement (estimated PLN 1B+ annually, no competitive bidding) is the most prominent governance concern in the wiki. Offset by: never sold a share, zero compensation, no CEO since 2020
- **[[wiki/companies/samsung-electronics|Samsung Electronics]]** — Lee family control via complex cross-shareholding structure; succession/inheritance tax controversies
- **[[wiki/companies/lg-corp|LG Corp]]** — Chaebol holding company structure with embedded discount
- **[[wiki/companies/jardine-matheson-holdings|Jardine Matheson]]** — Keswick family control; cross-shareholding with subsidiaries creating conglomerate discount

### VIE Structures
Chinese companies listed offshore via Variable Interest Entities face structural governance risk — investors own contractual rights to economic interest, not direct equity:
- [[wiki/companies/alibaba|Alibaba]], [[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]], [[wiki/companies/tencent|Tencent]], [[wiki/companies/full-truck-alliance|Full Truck Alliance]], [[wiki/companies/trip-com|Trip.com]], [[wiki/companies/netease|Netease]]

### SOE Influence
- **[[wiki/companies/china-mengniu-dairy|China Mengniu Dairy]]** — COFCO (state-owned) as controlling shareholder; potential tension between state objectives and minority shareholder interests
- **[[wiki/companies/china-resources-beer|China Resources Beer]]** — China Resources (state-owned) parentage

## Transparency Concerns

- **[[wiki/companies/dino-polska|Dino Polska]]** — PLN 32B company with no earnings calls, no CEO, communication exclusively through written reports. Factoring usage nearly doubled to PLN 2.2B in 2025 — buried in Note 26 of the annual report
- **[[wiki/companies/pdd-holdings|PDD Holdings]]** — Minimal management engagement with investors; Colin Huang's complete withdrawal from public role; malware allegations

## Notable Governance Strengths

For contrast, companies with exemplary governance:
- **[[wiki/companies/tsmc|TSMC]]** — 7/10 independent directors including former CEOs of BT Group, Applied Materials, Xilinx, and Xerox; transparent guidance culture
- **[[wiki/companies/dbs-group|DBS Group]]** — MAS-regulated with strong independent board; 15-year CEO tenure with deliberate succession planning
- **[[wiki/companies/hdfc-bank|HDFC Bank]]** — RBI oversight; clean audit history; systematic management development

## Assessment Framework

When evaluating governance concerns, consider:
1. **Is the misalignment priced in?** Samsung and LG trade at structural discounts
2. **Does the controlling shareholder's interest align with minority shareholders?** Biernacki's 51% stake at Dino creates alignment even if governance structure is opaque
3. **Is there a credible mechanism for change?** Korea's Corporate Value-up program may improve chaebol governance
4. **What's the worst-case scenario?** VIE structures could theoretically be voided by Chinese courts
