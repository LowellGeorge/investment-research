## Competitive Positioning

**TSMC** occupies an unprecedented monopolistic position in advanced foundry. 71% market share; sole manufacturer of chips for NVIDIA, AMD, Apple, and Qualcomm. The "customer trust covenant" (never designs own chips) is its most distinctive moat. Gross margins at 60%+ reflect true pricing power.

**Samsung Electronics** is the most vertically integrated semiconductor company globally — spanning DRAM, NAND, logic foundry, displays, and consumer electronics. Its foundry business operates at a loss, trailing TSMC by 1-2 generations in yields. Memory business is the profit engine, though cyclical.

**SK Hynix** has emerged as the AI memory champion through its HBM technology leadership. First-mover in HBM3E gave it supply allocation priority with NVIDIA. Memory pricing remains cyclical, but HBM provides structural differentiation and pricing power that commodity DRAM/NAND lack.

**MediaTek** is the leading fabless SoC designer for mobile, IoT, and increasingly automotive applications. Revenue ~$19B makes it a major customer for both TSMC and Samsung foundry. Edge AI capabilities and custom ASIC partnerships represent growth vectors.

## AI Value Chain Map

```
AI Chip Design (NVIDIA, AMD, Google)
    → Fabrication: TSMC (monopoly for leading-edge)
    → Memory: SK Hynix (HBM leader), Samsung (DRAM/NAND)
    → Packaging: TSMC CoWoS (bottleneck)
    → Servers: Quanta Computer (ODM)
    → Edge AI: MediaTek (mobile/IoT SoCs)
```

## Geopolitical Risk Spectrum

| Company | Primary Risk | Mitigation |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| TSMC | Taiwan Strait conflict; 90% of advanced capacity in Taiwan | Arizona, Japan, Germany fabs ($65B+ committed) |
| Samsung | South Korea geopolitics; China fab restrictions | Xi'an NAND fab; Austin logic fab; diversified geography |
| SK Hynix | China Wuxi/Dalian fab restrictions; export controls | Korea-based HBM production; Indiana fab planned |
| MediaTek | Cross-strait risk (Taiwan HQ); Huawei supply restrictions | Fabless model provides geographic flexibility |

## Key Insight

TSMC's 60% gross margin vs. Samsung Foundry's losses tells the entire story of this industry: scale, trust, and technology leadership create a natural monopoly in advanced chips. SK Hynix's HBM play creates a secondary monopoly position in AI memory. Samsung's strength is breadth, not depth in any single category. MediaTek is the fabless design survivor, having outlasted most competitors.
