## Strategic Positioning

**Nu Holdings** is the most valuable fintech in Latin America. David Velez built it from a single no-fee credit card to a 100M+ customer platform spanning banking, credit, insurance, and investment products across Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia. Customer acquisition cost of ~$5 via word-of-mouth is its most distinctive metric. The thesis: digital-first banking can achieve profitability at scale in emerging markets.

**Regional S.A.B.** is a Mexican regional bank focused on SME lending, agricultural finance, and the underserved Mexican middle market. Smaller scale but high-touch relationship banking in segments that large banks neglect. Mexico's nearshoring boom is a tailwind for its commercial lending franchise.

**Credicorp** is Peru's dominant financial conglomerate, anchored by BCP (Banco de Credito del Peru, founded 1889). Conservative underwriting culture in a politically volatile environment. Combines universal banking, insurance (Pacifico), and asset management (Prima AFP). The most traditional model in this comparison.

## The Valuation Puzzle

Nu at ~35-40x earnings vs. Credicorp at ~10-12x reflects the market's differentiation between growth and value in LatAm financial services. Key questions:

1. **Can Nu maintain 25%+ ROE as it scales credit?** The unsecured consumer lending book has not been tested through a severe Brazilian recession
2. **Will nearshoring lift Regional?** Mexico's manufacturing investment surge could drive sustained loan growth
3. **Is Peru's political risk overpriced?** Credicorp has navigated multiple political crises while maintaining ~18-20% ROE
