## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — state regulatory shock
- Risk #2 — CCI penalty crystallising materially
- Risk #3 — Indian consumer recession / affordability
- Risk #4 — aluminium + barley cost spikes
- Risk #5 — competitive intensification (AB InBev, Carlsberg)
- Risk #6 — productivity programme misses targets
- Sell triggers:
    - Gross margin <40% for two consecutive quarters
    - Premium volume growth <10% for two consecutive quarters
    - Market share losing for two consecutive quarters
    - CCI penalty crystallising at full INR 75bn level
    - Heineken stepping back from active management
    - Productivity delivering <half targeted savings
