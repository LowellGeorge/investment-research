## Q6 — Where is the market wrong?

- Variant #1 <> {{consensus prices on messy 2023-24 portfolio period; 2025 reshape clinically simplified + added premium brands; +89% NI growth is signal not noise}}
- Variant #2 <> {{Chipotle + Raising Cane's priced as marketing not real optionality; Mexican premium fast-casual significantly under-supplied vs US benchmarks}}
- Honest counter <> {{France + Argentina remain weak; South America rationalisation incomplete; smaller market cap = illiquidity discounts; net debt 2.5x reasonable but not low}}

## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — Mexican consumer slowdown
- Risk #2 — European consumer weakness (France, Spain)
- Risk #3 — FX volatility
- Risk #4 — Chipotle + Raising Cane's integration
- Risk #5 — debt refinancing pressure if rates shift
- Risk #6 — security events affecting operations
- Sell triggers:
    - Same-store sales turning negative for two consecutive quarters
    - Mexico revenue growth <5%
    - EBITDA margin compressing >100bps
    - Chipotle or Raising Cane's launches producing material losses
    - Net debt / EBITDA >3x
