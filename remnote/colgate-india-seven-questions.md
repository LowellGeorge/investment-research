## Q6 — Where is the market wrong?

- Variant #1 <> {{consensus views as slow-growth defensive; premium oral-care strong double-digit + rural channel recovery suggest growth potential higher than headline implies}}
- Variant #2 <> {{Visible White Purple Serum + oral-beauty innovation = category extension consensus models don't fully capture}}
- Honest counter <> {{Indian consumer goods regulatory complexity (GST + labour codes + tax structure) creates earnings volatility; premium multiple = no room for disappointment}}

## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — Indian consumer slowdown affecting premium oral-care
- Risk #2 — labour-code implementation costs higher than estimates
- Risk #3 — GST or tax-structure changes
- Risk #4 — competitive intensification (Patanjali, Dabur, international entrants)
- Risk #5 — raw material cost spikes
- Sell triggers:
    - Gross margin <65% for two consecutive quarters
    - Market share losing in toothpaste
    - Premium oral-care growth <5%
    - Net sales growth turning negative ex-GST disruption
    - Major distribution-channel issues affecting rural
