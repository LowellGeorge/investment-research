## Q6 — Where is the market wrong?

- Variant #1 <> {{consensus prices as generic Indonesian bank with country macro dominating; 85% CASA + 27% cost-to-income = world-class metrics putting BCA in same conversation as best global private banks; right comp is global premium banks not Indonesian peers}}
- Variant #2 <> {{AI-driven operational efficiency is structural multi-year compounding lever consensus models don't yet capture}}
- Honest counter <> {{rupiah + political risk persistent and structural; Hartono ownership creates strategic continuity AND concentration risk; even world-class franchise in Indonesia carries country discounts that may not close}}

## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — rupiah depreciation
- Risk #2 — loan growth deceleration + rising NPLs
- Risk #3 — political + policy instability
- Risk #4 — competitive intrusion from international or fintech
- Risk #5 — cyber + fraud risk
- Risk #6 — succession beyond current Hartono cycle
- Sell triggers:
    - CASA ratio <75% for two consecutive quarters
    - Cost-to-income >33% sustainably
    - NPL ratio >3% for two consecutive quarters
    - Capital adequacy <20% without explicit redeployment plan
    - NIM <4%
    - Major Hartono family ownership change
