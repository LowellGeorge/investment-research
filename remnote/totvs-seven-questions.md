# Totvs (TOTS3 BZ) — Seven Questions Flashcards

## The Seven Questions Frame

These flashcards are organised around the seven questions you ask of any investment. The question is the cognitive move you're training; the facts hang underneath. When reviewing, hold the parent question in mind — that's the superstructure that compounds across companies.

1. **What is it?** — what specifically is unique about this business that makes it irreplaceable?
2. **Can we trust them?** — how did management behave under specific past stress, not slogans
3. **Why does value compound?** — the durable engines of return, mechanism by mechanism
4. **What proves us right?** — specific KPIs you'd track to know
5. **What's it worth?** — building blocks and sensitivity, no false precision
6. **Where is the market wrong?** — variant perception plus the honest counter
7. **What could kill it / when do we sell?** — ranked risks plus specific exit triggers

**Anchor question for Totvs:** Q3 — the compounder mechanism (dominant ERP share × franchise distribution × cloud migration × Techfin/TaaS kicker) is what makes the thesis work. It is a Brazilian Constellation-in-the-making.
