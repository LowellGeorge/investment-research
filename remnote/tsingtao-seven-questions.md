- Variant #2 <> {{international localisation priced at zero; Tsingtao most globally-recognised Chinese beer brand; localised production in foreign markets opens multi-decade growth leg}}
- Honest counter <> {{Chinese beer category demand genuinely declining at volume level; premiumisation real but slower than execs forecast; competitive intensity rising; valuation discount partly justified by category headwinds}}

## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — Chinese beer category accelerating volume decline
- Risk #2 — raw material price spikes
- Risk #3 — weather + climate effects on consumption
- Risk #4 — competitive intensity (China Resources Beer, AB InBev, craft entrants)
- Risk #5 — international expansion execution
- Risk #6 — consumer shift to spirits or non-alcoholic
- Sell triggers:
    - Net profit margin <12% for two consecutive years
    - Sales volume declining >3% YoY
    - Dividend payout ratio cut materially
    - Major loss of market share in flagship Shandong region
    - Renewable-energy commitment reversing
