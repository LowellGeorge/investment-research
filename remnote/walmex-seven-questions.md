## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — Mexican consumer slowdown
- Risk #2 — competitive escalation from Soriana/Chedraui/OXXO
- Risk #3 — e-commerce execution in platform transition
- Risk #4 — Central America macro weakness
- Risk #5 — peso volatility
- Risk #6 — tax rate changes
- Sell triggers:
    - Same-store sales turning negative for two consecutive quarters
    - e-commerce growth <10%
    - EBITDA margin compressing >50bps for two consecutive quarters
    - Beneficios loyalty active user growth stalling
    - Major management transitions disrupting execution
