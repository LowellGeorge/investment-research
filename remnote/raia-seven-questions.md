# Raia Drogasil (RADL3 BZ) — Seven Questions Flashcards

## The Seven Questions Frame

These flashcards are organised around the seven questions you ask of any investment. The question is the cognitive move you're training; the facts hang underneath.

1. **What is it?** 2. **Can we trust them?** 3. **Why does value compound?** 4. **What proves us right?** 5. **What's it worth?** 6. **Where is the market wrong?** 7. **What could kill it / when do we sell?**

**Anchor question for Raia Drogasil:** Q3 — the compounding mechanism (store rollout into consolidating market × digital flywheel × GLP-1 leadership × São Paulo concentration kicker).
