## Q7 — What could kill it / when do we sell?

- Risk #1 — global IT services demand slowdown
- Risk #2 — Vietnamese dong depreciation
- Risk #3 — AI-driven productivity reducing offshore-services demand
- Risk #4 — succession beyond Truong Gia Binh
- Risk #5 — competitive intensification (Indian IT, Chinese-SEA competitors)
- Risk #6 — data-centre capital intensity rising materially
- Sell triggers:
    - Global IT services revenue growth <10% for two consecutive quarters
    - Japan market growth <15%
    - ROE <16% for two consecutive years
    - "Made-by-FPT" product growth turning negative
    - Major customer-mandate loss (Airbus or comparable)
    - Truong Gia Binh steps back without named credible successor
