# Sea Limited — The Seven Questions

Sea is the fifteenth-largest position in the GEM portfolio at three-point-one percent, with a market cap of roughly one hundred billion US dollars. Here is Sea in seven questions.

---

## Cold open

Sea just closed 2025 with full-year revenue of twenty-three billion US dollars, up thirty-six percent. Net income of one-point-six billion, up from four hundred and forty-eight million in 2024 — a nearly four-fold increase. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of three-point-four billion, up seventy-five percent. Shopee serves four hundred million active buyers. Monee, the fintech arm, has a loan book above nine billion US dollars, up eighty percent, with a ninety-day non-performing loan ratio of one-point-one percent. Garena averages a hundred million daily players and Free Fire bookings nearly doubled from 2023 levels.

All three legs — commerce, fintech, games — working at the same time, with a ten-billion-dollar-plus gross cash position. The question is no longer whether Sea works; it is how far this specific combination of businesses can compound before any one of them breaks.

Seven questions.

---

## Question One: What is it?

Sea is the dominant digital consumer platform of Southeast Asia, a Singapore-headquartered company with three businesses stacked on one user.

The first business is Shopee — the e-commerce marketplace. Full-year gross merchandise value of one hundred and twenty-seven billion US dollars, up twenty-seven percent. Four hundred million active buyers and twenty million sellers across Southeast Asia, Taiwan, and a growing footprint in Brazil. Shopee's full-year adjusted profit was eight hundred and eighty million US dollars, up from one hundred and fifty-six million in 2024 — that is a five-and-a-half-times step-up in one year, the sharpest profit inflection in Asian consumer internet.

The second business is Monee — the fintech arm, formerly known as SeaMoney. The loan book has crossed nine billion US dollars, up eighty percent. Thirty-seven million active credit users, up forty percent. Off-platform consumer credit, the SPayLater product, grew three hundred percent. Ninety-day non-performing loan ratio is one-point-one percent. Average loan per user is two hundred and forty dollars, up twenty-seven percent. Full-year adjusted profit was just over one billion US dollars, up forty-three percent.

The third business is Garena — the gaming publisher. Garena runs Free Fire, one of the world's most-played mobile games, and a growing portfolio of collaborations and esports events. Full-year bookings of two-point-nine billion, up thirty-seven percent. A hundred million daily players. Free Fire bookings nearly doubled from 2023 levels and the Free Fire World Series set a Guinness World Record for the largest mobile team-based esports tournament in 2025.

The thing to internalise: these are three separate businesses operating on one user base and one capital pool, and all three are simultaneously growing double-digits and throwing off cash. That combination is structurally rare.

## Question Two: Can we trust them?

Yes — and the 2022 reset is the single clearest evidence.

Forrest Li founded Sea in 2009 and is still the Chief Executive; he, Tony Hou and Gang Ye are the founding trio. They took the company public in 2017 on the New York Stock Exchange.

The past-stress case that matters is 2022. Sea had been burning cash to grow Shopee in dozens of markets simultaneously — India, Europe, Latin America, Africa. Tiger Global and the growth-at-any-cost narrative was the external pressure. In late 2022 the stock collapsed more than eighty percent from its peak. Management did a clean pivot: they shuttered Shopee's unprofitable markets — India, Europe, parts of Latin America — laid off aggressively across gaming and commerce, and told the Street they were shifting to profitable growth. Three years later the proof is in the numbers: Shopee's profit base went from one hundred and fifty-six million to eight hundred and eighty million US dollars in twelve months, and the company has generated five billion US dollars of operating cash flow in 2025 alone. That is what discipline looks like after a cycle.

The capital discipline signals from the Q4 2025 print are consistent. Gross cash position above ten billion US dollars throughout 2025, ending at ten-and-a-half billion. A one-billion-dollar share repurchase programme is in place, with purchases in the fourth quarter. The Monee loan book grew eighty percent while the non-performing loan ratio stayed at one-point-one percent — underwriting discipline scaling with growth. Garena is monetising without chasing the latest games fashion. Shopee guided twenty-five percent gross merchandise value growth in 2026 with adjusted profit at least flat — telling the market plainly that they will reinvest any operating leverage gains back into the business.

The watch item is the founder-centric governance. Forrest Li and the founding team hold super-voting shares. Succession depth outside Li, Hou and Ye has not been publicly tested, and the next five years will include the first genuine operating-leadership handovers.

## Question Three: Why does value compound?

Three engines, plus a kicker.

The first engine is Shopee's logistics and monetisation flywheel. Shopee's take rate is twelve-point-four percent on gross merchandise value. Advertising revenue grew seventy percent year-on-year in 2025. The VIP programme doubled to seven million subscribers in the fourth quarter. Shopee's long-term adjusted profit margin target is two to three percent of gross merchandise value; the FY 2025 number was zero-point-seven percent. On a hundred-and-twenty-seven-billion-dollar base, each one-point of margin improvement is more than a billion dollars of profit. That is the operating leverage story still ahead.

The second engine is Monee's credit and embedded-finance compounder. This is the classic Sea flywheel inside a bank-adjacent structure: Shopee generates transactions, those transactions generate credit demand, credit is underwritten with platform data, the resulting profits fund further growth. The off-platform lending product grew three hundred percent in 2025 — meaning Monee is now a credible standalone credit business, not just a Shopee payments tool. Loan book compounding at eighty percent with one-point-one percent non-performing loans is a rare unit-economics combination.

The third engine is Garena's cash machine running underneath it all. Garena's adjusted profit of one-point-seven billion US dollars is the funding source that quietly underwrote the rebuild of Shopee and the growth of Monee. Free Fire is durable, monetises globally, and has been extending its lifecycle through IP collaborations including NARUTO and Squid Game. This is a stable cash flywheel, not a declining legacy business, which is the consensus framing.

The kicker is Brazil. Shopee is now gaining share in Brazil against MercadoLibre and Amazon, narrowing the average-order-value gap and rolling out the VIP programme into the Brazilian market in 2026. Brazil adds a second major growth region to Southeast Asia without Sea having to buy anything — the platform, logistics, and fintech stack are exportable. If Brazil ramps to anything close to Shopee's Southeast-Asia unit economics, the addressable market expands dramatically.

## Question Four: What proves us right, and what to track?

Six specific metrics.

First, Shopee's adjusted profit margin as a percentage of gross merchandise value — zero-point-seven percent in 2025; progression toward the long-term target of two to three percent is the re-rating test. Second, Monee's non-performing loan ratio at the ninety-day mark — one-point-one percent today; holding below one-point-five percent through the loan-book compounding is the credit-discipline test. Third, Garena's bookings growth — thirty-seven percent in 2025; sustained double-digit growth confirms the gaming leg is a durable cash machine not a fading legacy. Fourth, Brazil gross merchandise value growth and average-order-value gap versus MercadoLibre — the Brazilian leg scaling is the kicker. Fifth, Shopee advertising revenue as a percentage of total — seventy percent growth in 2025; mix shift toward ads re-rates margins. Sixth, cash generation — five billion US dollars of operating cash flow in 2025; this should keep expanding as the businesses mature.

## Question Five: What's it worth?

The honest framing. Full-year 2025 net income was one-point-six billion US dollars. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation was three-point-four billion, up seventy-five percent. Gross cash position is ten-and-a-half billion; operating cash flow was five billion. Market cap at time of writing is around a hundred to a hundred and ten billion US dollars.

On headline multiples the stock trades at roughly sixty to seventy times trailing net income and closer to thirty times trailing adjusted profit. That looks rich in isolation. But the Shopee margin gap — zero-point-seven percent today versus a two-to-three percent long-term target — means the earnings trajectory over three to five years is potentially multiples higher than the current trailing number. On a forward basis, if Shopee hits even the low end of its margin target while Monee keeps compounding, the stock can trade at a mid-twenties forward price-to-earnings on 2027 numbers.

The simple framing: a genuine three-legged compounder in the fastest-growing region of the world, with one leg still at the early-innings of margin expansion, one leg compounding loan book at eighty percent with one-percent non-performing loans, and one leg generating cash. Comps: MercadoLibre in Latin America trades at a higher forward multiple with a single commerce-plus-fintech model. Sea has more legs, similar growth, and comparable unit economics.

The key sensitivity is Shopee's take-rate and competitive intensity. TikTok Shop, Lazada and Tokopedia are all investing. If Shopee has to spend harder to defend share rather than expanding margins, the re-rating thesis delays.

## Question Six: Where is the market wrong?

Two variant perception angles.

First: Sea is a three-business compounder that the market still prices as a cyclical commerce story. When sell-side models average out the Shopee margin path, the Monee credit compounder, and the Garena cash base, the forward earnings trajectory looks linear. The reality is non-linear — each of the three legs has a discrete re-rating catalyst in the next eighteen months. Shopee crossing one percent of GMV in adjusted margin, Monee breaking above one-and-a-half billion in adjusted profit, Garena sustaining double-digit bookings growth past the Free Fire lifecycle risk. Any two of those three happening simultaneously is a re-rating event.

Second: the Brazil leg is treated as a distraction, when it is actually the natural next market for the Shopee playbook. Brazil is a mobile-first, under-banked, logistics-challenged consumer market — precisely where Shopee's Southeast-Asia playbook works. MercadoLibre already operates at fifteen-percent-plus margins in Brazil; Shopee taking share while narrowing the margin gap is the quiet thesis the market underweights.

The honest counter: a severe regional recession in Southeast Asia or a sharp Indonesian consumer slowdown would compress all three legs at once, because the user base overlaps. Concentration risk is real. And the Garena pipeline beyond Free Fire remains the single biggest unknown — another hit title is not guaranteed and the market has been burned on new game launches before.

## Question Seven: What could kill it, and when do we sell?

The real risks, ranked.

One: Monee credit cycle. An eighty-percent-growth loan book is the largest earnings-risk concentration inside Sea. If the Southeast-Asian consumer weakens, non-performing loans rise and the fintech leg takes the profit hit first. Two: Shopee competitive escalation. TikTok Shop and Lazada investing aggressively in logistics and subsidies in Indonesia and Thailand could force Shopee into renewed discounting. Three: Free Fire lifecycle. The single-title concentration inside Garena — Free Fire is the overwhelming majority of gaming profit — makes Garena vulnerable to any erosion in Free Fire daily active users or average revenue per paying user. Four: Brazilian execution. Logistics, tax, and payment-stack complexity have historically broken foreign entrants. Shopee in Brazil is gaining but has not yet reached scale. Five: founder concentration. Forrest Li, Tony Hou and Gang Ye are the spine. A two-of-three departure in twelve months would genuinely damage strategic coherence. Six: Southeast Asian regulatory tightening. Digital lending regulation in Indonesia, Vietnam and Malaysia could cap Monee's runway.

Specific sell triggers. Monee ninety-day non-performing loan ratio rising above two percent for two consecutive quarters — credit discipline breaking. Shopee gross merchandise value growth decelerating below fifteen percent for two consecutive quarters — the competitive flywheel stalling. Garena bookings declining year-on-year for two consecutive quarters — Free Fire lifecycle concern materialising. Shopee adjusted profit margin as a percentage of GMV stalling or declining for a full year — the long-term margin path broken. Li, Hou or Ye leaving without a named credible successor. A major Indonesian or Vietnamese regulation capping digital-lending interest rates below current levels.

## Sign-off

One company, seven questions, twelve minutes. Sea is the Southeast-Asian three-legged compounder — commerce, fintech, games — where the operating discipline of the 2022 reset is now paying compound returns, and the Shopee margin path is the single biggest variant perception opportunity. The next print, first-quarter 2026 on the twelfth of May, tests whether Shopee's 2026 profit-flat guide is credible alongside the twenty-five-percent GMV growth, and whether Monee's non-performing loan ratio holds as the loan book keeps compounding. Watch those two, plus the Brazil signals, in that order.
