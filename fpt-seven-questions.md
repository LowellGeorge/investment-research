# FPT Corporation — The Seven Questions

FPT Corporation is the thirty-third-largest position in the GEM portfolio at zero-point-eight percent, with a market cap of around eight billion US dollars. Here is FPT in seven questions.

---

## Cold open

FPT Corporation just closed 2025 with revenue of seventy trillion Vietnamese dong, up nearly twelve percent year-on-year, and profit after tax of eleven-point-two trillion dong, up nineteen percent. Earnings per share rose twenty-one percent. Return on equity reached twenty-one-point-four percent. Global IT services revenue grew fourteen percent, with the Japanese market alone growing twenty-five percent. The company signed two record-breaking digital transformation contracts in 2025 — two hundred and fifty-six million US dollars and one hundred million US dollars in Asia and the United States. FPT became Airbus's global IT partner, entered strategic partnerships with Chelsea Football Club and Clearlake Capital, inaugurated one of Vietnam's largest data centres, and secured exclusive English Premier League broadcasting rights in Vietnam.

This is the dominant Vietnamese technology platform compounding at near-twenty-percent return on equity with global digital-transformation contracts at unprecedented scale.

Seven questions.

---

## Question One: What is it?

FPT Corporation is the largest technology company in Vietnam by revenue, profit, and market capitalisation, with a portfolio spanning global IT services, domestic IT services, telecommunications, online advertising, education, and investments.

The Global IT Services segment is the largest by both revenue and growth. Revenue reached thirty-five trillion dong in 2025, up fourteen percent. Profit before tax was five-and-a-half trillion dong, also up fourteen-and-a-half percent. The Japanese market is the largest geographic contributor, growing twenty-five percent. The United States and European Union markets are scaling rapidly through partnerships with major industrial clients including Airbus.

Telecommunications Services contributed nineteen trillion dong in revenue, up eleven percent, with profit before tax up twenty-two percent. This is the domestic Vietnamese broadband and pay-television franchise, alongside data-centre infrastructure.

Domestic IT Services contributed nine trillion dong in revenue, up eleven-and-a-half percent. The "Made-by-FPT" proprietary product portfolio grew nineteen percent. Online Advertising contributed eight hundred billion dong, up fourteen percent. Education, Investment & Others — including FPT University, edge-of-business venture investments, and emerging segments — contributed seven trillion dong of revenue with profit before tax up twenty-four percent.

The thing to internalise: FPT is not just a Vietnamese tech company. It is the dominant technology compounder of Vietnam, scaling globally through Japan and the United States, with proprietary products, telecommunications infrastructure, and education adding diversified compounding legs.

## Question Two: Can we trust them?

Yes — and the multi-decade execution under founder Trương Gia Bình is the evidence.

Truong Gia Binh founded FPT in 1988 and has shepherded the company from a Vietnamese systems-integrator startup into a globally relevant IT-services and telecommunications group. His strategic continuity is unusual in emerging-markets technology leadership.

The capital discipline signals from the 2025 print are exceptional. Revenue up nearly twelve percent, profit up nineteen percent, earnings per share up twenty-one percent, return on equity at twenty-one-and-a-half percent. Cash position grew from nine-and-a-third trillion to ten-and-a-half trillion dong year-on-year. The acquisition agenda is disciplined and strategic: sixty-five-and-a-half percent of Nam Viet Software Solutions, one hundred percent of FPT Wallet, all operations of David Lamm Consulting in Germany. Each acquisition specifically expands capability in adjacent areas.

The strategic-partnership signals are equally striking. Becoming Airbus's global IT partner is a quality mandate that few Vietnamese companies could win. Chelsea Football Club and Clearlake Capital partnerships demonstrate global brand and capital relationships. Inauguration of one of Vietnam's largest data centres positions FPT for the artificial-intelligence and cloud infrastructure wave. Exclusive English Premier League broadcasting rights in Vietnam adds consumer-facing brand reach.

The watch item is succession. Truong Gia Binh has been the founder-leader for over thirty-five years; succession planning becomes a multi-year quality test.

## Question Three: Why does value compound?

Three engines plus a kicker.

The first engine is global IT services scaling, particularly through Japan. Vietnam-based engineering talent is competitively priced, English-and-Japanese capable, and increasingly skilled in artificial-intelligence and cloud transformation work. As global enterprises diversify supply chains away from concentration in single locations, FPT captures share. Japanese demand grew twenty-five percent in 2025; United States and European demand are scaling.

The second engine is the Vietnamese telecommunications and infrastructure franchise. Telecommunications profit before tax grew twenty-two percent in 2025. Broadband, pay-television, and the data-centre infrastructure are durable cash generators with multi-decade Vietnamese consumer growth ahead.

The third engine is the proprietary product portfolio. "Made-by-FPT" products grew nineteen percent — these are genuine intellectual-property assets that scale globally rather than being client-specific service revenue.

The kicker is the artificial-intelligence and data-centre infrastructure investment. The newly inaugurated data centre is one of Vietnam's largest. As AI workloads scale globally, Vietnamese-located data-centre capacity with appropriate power, network, and cost structure becomes increasingly valuable.

## Question Four: What proves us right, and what to track?

Six metrics. Global IT services revenue growth — fourteen percent, sustained double-digit confirms the scaling. Japan market growth — twenty-five percent, watch for continued progression. Return on equity — twenty-one-and-a-half percent, sustained above twenty confirms compounder quality. Domestic telecommunications profit growth — twenty-two percent, sustained double-digit confirms the franchise. "Made-by-FPT" product growth — nineteen percent, watch for product-revenue mix shift. Mega-contract wins — record contracts in 2025; watch for continued large enterprise wins.

## Question Five: What's it worth?

Full-year 2025 profit after tax was eleven-point-two trillion dong, roughly four hundred and fifty million US dollars. Earnings per share was seven thousand eight hundred and fifty-seven dong. Return on equity was twenty-one-and-a-half percent. Market cap is around eight billion US dollars. Trading multiples sit at premium levels for emerging-markets technology — generally low-to-mid-twenty times trailing earnings.

The simple framing: a dominant Vietnamese technology compounder with global IT services scaling at fourteen percent growth, telecommunications and education adding diversified earnings, and twenty-percent-plus return on equity. Comparable set: Indian IT services like Infosys, TCS, Wipro at higher multiples for similar return profiles; Vietnamese consumer leaders at similar or higher multiples. Vietnam's emerging-markets discount is the structural drag.

The key sensitivity is global IT services demand. A sharper US or European technology spending slowdown compresses the highest-margin growth segment.

## Question Six: Where is the market wrong?

First variant: consensus treats FPT as a Vietnamese local technology company; the right framing is a globally-scaling IT services and infrastructure compounder where Japan, the US, and Europe are real growth markets. The Airbus, Chelsea, and Clearlake partnerships are not marketing — they are quality mandates. Second: the data-centre and AI-infrastructure investment positions FPT for the AI wave; Vietnamese-located data-centre capacity with good power and cost structure becomes increasingly strategic.

The honest counter: Vietnamese capital markets remain illiquid and country-discounted. Currency volatility affects USD returns. Founder concentration creates real key-person risk.

## Question Seven: What could kill it, and when do we sell?

Risks: global IT services demand slowdown; Vietnamese dong depreciation; AI-driven productivity reducing offshore-services demand; succession beyond Truong Gia Binh; competitive intensification from Indian IT and Chinese-South-East-Asian competitors; data-centre capital intensity rising materially.

Sell triggers: global IT services revenue growth decelerating below ten percent for two consecutive quarters; Japan market growth decelerating below fifteen percent; return on equity falling below sixteen percent for two consecutive years; "Made-by-FPT" product growth turning negative; major customer-mandate loss like Airbus or comparable. Truong Gia Binh stepping back without a named credible successor.

One company, seven questions. FPT Corporation is the dominant Vietnamese technology compounder scaling globally through Japan and the United States, with telecommunications, education, and AI-data-centre infrastructure adding diversified compounding. The next print is the first-quarter 2026 result on the seventh of May. Watch global IT services growth, Japan market progression, and major-contract wins.
