# Indian top 20 ownership cases

This mirrors the earlier internal-style format, but with the evidence sections pushed harder on what the actual advantage is, how the unit economics are moving, and whether the moat looks to be widening, merely holding, or starting to narrow.

## Ticker assumptions

The Bloomberg-style mnemonics BOS IN, CLGT IN, BAF IN, and TMCV IN were treated as Bosch Limited, Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited, Bajaj Finance, and the Tata Motors commercial-vehicle listing respectively, based on market references for those tickers ([NSE India](https://www.nseindia.com/get-quote/equity/BOSCHLTD/Bosch-Limited)) ([Bosch in India](https://www.bosch.in/our-company/shareholder-information/)) ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/CLGT:IN)) ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/baf:in:equity-capital-and-reserves)) ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/TMCV:IN)) ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/TMCV-IN)) ([NSE India](https://www.nseindia.com/get-quotes/equity?symbol=TMCV)).

If your internal map for TMCV or BAF points to a different listed line, that section is the only one that may need a swap; the rest map cleanly to the intended Indian equities ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/TMCV:IN)) ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/baf:in:equity-capital-and-reserves)).

## HDFC Bank

- Pitch: HDFC Bank is still the cleanest core India financials compounding story in the list: a scaled private bank with a powerful deposit franchise, broad retail and wholesale reach, and the balance-sheet depth to keep taking share across cycles ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)).
- Evidence: The real edge is low-cost funding, branch density, and cross-sell breadth rather than any single product line, which is why the bank could grow revenue from INR 1.98 trillion in FY2023 to INR 4.19 trillion in FY2025 while still generating INR 861.5 billion of operating profit and more than INR 1.12 trillion of free-cash-flow proxy in FY2025 ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)). The important nuance is that the franchise is still massive and productive, but the moat looks more absorbed by merger integration than visibly widening right now: operating margin fell from 33.4% in FY2023 to 20.5% in FY2025 and net margin fell from 25.0% to 16.1%, so scale remains intact but incremental economics have not yet returned to the old HDFC Bank standard ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)).
- Debate: The live question is whether deposit costs and post-merger integration keep returns below the old HDFC Bank standard for longer than bulls expect ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)).

## Kotak Mahindra

- Pitch: Kotak Mahindra Bank is the high-quality, conservative private-bank holding where the case rests on franchise quality first and growth re-acceleration second ([Kotak Mahindra Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KOTAKBANK.NS)).
- Evidence: Kotak’s advantage is that it monetizes prudence unusually well: the balance sheet is still conservatively run, but the franchise keeps converting that conservatism into growth and better earnings quality, with revenue compounding from INR 662.7 billion in FY2023 to INR 1.05 trillion in FY2025 and net income rising from INR 149.3 billion to INR 221.3 billion over the same period ([Kotak Mahindra Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KOTAKBANK.NS)). Even after some FY2024 pressure, operating margin recovered to 27.7% in FY2025 and net margin was 21.2%, while the bank ended with only modest net debt relative to its balance-sheet scale, which suggests the moat is stable to slowly widening through underwriting discipline and liability quality rather than through aggressive balance-sheet stretch ([Kotak Mahindra Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KOTAKBANK.NS)).
- Debate: The bear case is that the premium quality is well understood already, so the stock only really works if loan growth and operating leverage visibly improve ([Kotak Mahindra Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KOTAKBANK.NS)).

## ICICI Bank

- Pitch: ICICI Bank is the more obvious execution-led private-bank compounder: strong retail mix, cleaner underwriting, and proven ability to convert scale into earnings growth ([ICICI Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIBANK.NS)).
- Evidence: ICICI’s advantage is now execution density rather than simple size: retail banking breadth, product coverage, and cleaner underwriting have let revenue climb from INR 1.86 trillion in FY2023 to INR 2.94 trillion in FY2025 while operating profit more than doubled from INR 354.6 billion to INR 730.0 billion and net income rose to INR 510.3 billion ([ICICI Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIBANK.NS)). The moat looks strong but not obviously widening in the latest year because margins peaked earlier in the recovery: operating margin reached 26.1% in FY2024 before easing to 24.9% in FY2025, and net margin similarly eased to 17.4%, so the franchise still looks better than it did two years ago, just no longer on a straight-line upward rerating in underlying economics ([ICICI Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIBANK.NS)).
- Debate: The main pushback is simply that it is no longer misunderstood, so future upside depends more on sustaining superior execution than on rerating alone ([ICICI Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIBANK.NS)).

## Bharti Airtel

- Pitch: Bharti Airtel is a structural telecom and digital-infrastructure story rather than a pure cyclical telecom trade, with India mobile pricing power, enterprise assets, and Africa optionality all inside one platform ([Bharti Airtel finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BHARTIARTL.NS)).
- Evidence: Airtel’s moat is network scale plus pricing discipline: once the network is built, traffic and ARPU gains fall through at very high incremental returns, which is why revenue rose from INR 1.39 trillion in FY2023 to INR 1.73 trillion in FY2025 and operating cash flow reached INR 813.2 billion in FY2025 ([Bharti Airtel finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BHARTIARTL.NS)). Operating margin has been broadly resilient around the mid-20s, while net margin jumped from 6.0% in FY2023 to 19.4% in FY2025, so the moat looks to be widening again at the equity-holder level if tariff discipline and mix improvement continue, even though leverage still makes the capital structure look heavy in absolute terms ([Bharti Airtel finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BHARTIARTL.NS)).
- Debate: The argument against it is that a lot of tariff optimism is already in the stock, so future returns need continued ARPU improvement and disciplined competition ([Bharti Airtel finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BHARTIARTL.NS)).

## ICICI Lombard

- Pitch: ICICI Lombard is one of the best non-life insurance assets in India because it combines underwriting quality, distribution breadth, and long runway from still-low insurance penetration ([ICICI Lombard finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIGI.NS)).
- Evidence: The source of advantage is not just brand, but a broad distribution footprint across agents, brokers, bancassurance, partnerships, and digital channels, which gives the company room to grow without buying business irresponsibly ([ICICI Lombard finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIGI.NS)). That shows up in the numbers: revenue compounded from INR 185.7 billion in FY2023 to INR 249.0 billion in FY2025, net income rose from INR 17.3 billion to INR 25.1 billion, and operating margin expanded from 11.4% to 13.3%, so this does look like a moat that is gradually widening because growth is being converted into better underwriting economics rather than diluted by weaker pricing ([ICICI Lombard finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIGI.NS)).
- Debate: The risk is that insurance can always look optically expensive, and any slippage in claims or pricing discipline can compress the premium rating fast ([ICICI Lombard finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIGI.NS)).

## Bosch

- Pitch: Bosch is the India auto-content and localization story through a high-quality engineering supplier, which means it benefits when vehicle technology content rises even if headline auto volumes are only moderate ([Bosch finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BOSCHLTD.NS)).
- Evidence: Bosch’s advantage is engineering intensity and high-value content per vehicle, not just exposure to auto units, which is why it can still earn operating margins north of 26% in India despite being tied to a cyclical end market ([Bosch finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BOSCHLTD.NS)). Revenue rose from INR 147.4 billion in FY2023 to INR 178.9 billion in FY2025 and operating profit rose from INR 38.7 billion to INR 46.9 billion, but net margin has been choppy, moving from 9.7% in FY2023 to 15.1% in FY2024 and back to 11.3% in FY2025, so the moat looks real and defensible at the operating level but not clearly widening because below-the-line earnings still swing with cycle and mix ([Bosch finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BOSCHLTD.NS)).
- Debate: The hesitation is valuation, because the stock already prices in quality, while near-term earnings still move around with the auto cycle and customer mix ([Bosch finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BOSCHLTD.NS)).

## KEI Industries

- Pitch: KEI is a clean way to own India electrification, construction, and premiumization in wires and cables, with organized share gains as the central thesis ([KEI Industries finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KEI.NS)).
- Evidence: KEI’s edge is brand plus distribution in a category that is steadily formalizing, so organized players can keep gaining share even without heroic end-market assumptions ([KEI Industries finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KEI.NS)). Revenue compounded from INR 68.5 billion in FY2023 to INR 96.1 billion in FY2025 and net income from INR 4.8 billion to INR 7.0 billion, while operating margin stayed tightly in the 9.6% to 9.9% range and net margin edged up to 7.2%, which says the moat is probably widening slowly through share gains and mix rather than through a dramatic margin step-up yet ([KEI Industries finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KEI.NS)).
- Debate: The challenge is that the stock is already priced like a high-confidence compounder, so any slowdown in capex demand or margin pressure matters disproportionately ([KEI Industries finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/KEI.NS)).

## HCL Technologies

- Pitch: HCLTech is the cash-generative Indian IT services name with enough exposure to infrastructure, engineering, and products to look different from a plain vanilla outsourcing business ([HCLTech finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HCLTECH.NS)).
- Evidence: HCLTech’s advantage is that it is more diversified than a classic labor-arbitrage outsourcer, with infrastructure management, engineering, and products helping protect margins and cash conversion when discretionary IT spending slows ([HCLTech finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HCLTECH.NS)). Revenue rose from INR 1.03 trillion in FY2023 to INR 1.18 trillion in FY2025, net income rose from INR 150.9 billion to INR 174.4 billion, operating margin held almost perfectly flat around 18.2%, and FY2025 free cash flow was still about INR 224.2 billion, so this is a stable moat story rather than a widening-moat story, but stability at that level is exactly why it belongs in a quality book ([HCLTech finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HCLTECH.NS)).
- Debate: The question is whether that mix premium is enough to offset a still-muted global IT demand backdrop and slower discretionary spending ([HCLTech finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HCLTECH.NS)).

## State Bank

- Pitch: SBI is the scale public-sector bank that gives you direct exposure to India’s credit cycle, but with much better profitability and balance-sheet credibility than the old PSU-bank stereotype ([State Bank of India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/SBIN.NS)).
- Evidence: SBI’s advantage is simply franchise breadth that no private competitor can replicate easily: distribution, deposits, government linkage, and balance-sheet scale all matter, but what changed is that the franchise is now monetizing those advantages more efficiently ([State Bank of India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/SBIN.NS)). Revenue grew from INR 4.60 trillion in FY2023 to INR 6.50 trillion in FY2025, operating profit rose from INR 754.3 billion to INR 1.06 trillion, net income rose from INR 556.5 billion to INR 775.6 billion, and margins remained healthy, with operating margin back to 16.4% in FY2025 and net margin 11.9%, so the moat looks like it is still widening from a low historical base even if the market remains slower to fully trust a PSU bank franchise ([State Bank of India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/SBIN.NS)).
- Debate: The reason some investors still hesitate is that the state ownership overhang and lower-quality historical memory can cap the multiple even when earnings are strong ([State Bank of India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/SBIN.NS)).

## Maruti Suzuki

- Pitch: Maruti is still the cleanest way to own Indian passenger-vehicle volume depth, first-time buyer exposure, and distribution strength, especially if the domestic auto cycle remains healthy ([Maruti Suzuki finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/MARUTI.NS)).
- Evidence: Maruti’s moat is domestic scale, dealer reach, and service infrastructure, which matter enormously in mass-market autos because they lower customer-acquisition cost and support resale value as well as market share ([Maruti Suzuki finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/MARUTI.NS)). Revenue increased from INR 1.18 trillion in FY2023 to INR 1.53 trillion in FY2025, operating profit rose from INR 251.3 billion to INR 351.4 billion, and net income rose sharply to INR 145.0 billion, but the margin story has plateaued after the post-supply-chain rebound, with operating margin at 23.0% in FY2025 versus 23.2% in FY2024 and net margin basically flat at 9.5%, so the moat looks strong and well-defended rather than clearly widening today ([Maruti Suzuki finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/MARUTI.NS)).
- Debate: The debate is whether the company can keep defending share and pricing as the SUV mix shifts and competition intensifies higher up the value curve ([Maruti Suzuki finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/MARUTI.NS)).

## Oberoi Realty

- Pitch: Oberoi Realty is the scarce Mumbai premium real-estate developer where the thesis is less about generic housing beta and more about asset quality, execution, and embedded annuity value ([Oberoi Realty finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/OBEROIRLTY.NS)).
- Evidence: Oberoi’s advantage is scarcity value in premium micro-markets plus a reputation for execution, which is why it can earn economics that ordinary developers simply do not come close to ([Oberoi Realty finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/OBEROIRLTY.NS)). Revenue increased from INR 41.7 billion in FY2023 to INR 52.4 billion in FY2025, operating margin expanded from 49.6% to 57.5%, and net margin stayed extraordinary even after a slight slip from FY2024, landing at 42.5% in FY2025, so the moat looks stable to modestly widening because pricing power and project execution still show up directly in elite margins ([Oberoi Realty finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/OBEROIRLTY.NS)).
- Debate: The risk is that property stocks always carry timing risk, so if luxury demand or launch momentum pauses, the market can turn quickly on sentiment ([Oberoi Realty finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/OBEROIRLTY.NS)).

## Colgate India

- Pitch: Colgate India is the classic category-leader compounder, with oral-care leadership, deep distribution, and scope to keep premiumizing rather than relying only on category growth ([Colgate India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/COLPAL.NS)).
- Evidence: The advantage is textbook FMCG moat: category-leading brand recall, shelf presence, and distribution muscle let Colgate keep pricing and premiumizing in a slow-growing category without losing profitability ([Colgate India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/COLPAL.NS)). Revenue moved from INR 51.9 billion in FY2023 to INR 60.0 billion in FY2025, net income climbed from INR 10.5 billion to INR 14.4 billion, operating margin stayed around 30%, net margin improved from 20.2% to 24.0%, and the business ended with net cash, so the moat looks at least intact and probably inching wider through premium mix rather than through headline volume acceleration ([Colgate India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/COLPAL.NS)).
- Debate: The pushback is simple: this is a high-quality defensive business, but it is not an obviously cheap one, so growth has to stay resilient to justify the premium ([Colgate India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/COLPAL.NS)).

## Niva Bupa

- Pitch: Niva Bupa is the pure-play health-insurance penetration story in India, so the attraction is the long runway and category structure more than current-period earnings optics ([Niva Bupa finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/NIVABUPA.NS)).
- Evidence: The advantage is not yet a mature moat so much as an emerging one: a focused health-insurance platform, brand association, and specialist distribution give it a chance to build scale in a market that is still underpenetrated ([Niva Bupa finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/NIVABUPA.NS)). The current annual filing available shows revenue of INR 56.9 billion, operating margin of 7.4%, net margin of 3.8%, and operating cash flow of INR 16.3 billion, which is enough to show the model can produce profits and cash, but not enough yet to say the moat is proven or widening; this is still an underwriting and scale-conversion story in formation ([Niva Bupa finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/NIVABUPA.NS)).
- Debate: The stock only works if the market keeps believing in the long-duration health-insurance growth story, because on near-term earnings it already looks very expensive ([Niva Bupa finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/NIVABUPA.NS)).

## Bajaj Auto

- Pitch: Bajaj Auto is a disciplined two-wheeler and exports compounder, with capital allocation credibility and optionality from electric two-wheelers layered on top ([Bajaj Auto finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJAJ-AUTO.NS)).
- Evidence: Bajaj Auto’s edge is efficient manufacturing plus export franchise strength, which gives it better structural profitability than many auto peers even when product cycles move around ([Bajaj Auto finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJAJ-AUTO.NS)). Revenue compounded from INR 373.3 billion in FY2023 to INR 518.6 billion in FY2025 and operating margin improved from 23.1% to 26.9%, which is exactly what you want to see if the core operating moat is strengthening; the caveat is that net margin fell to 14.1% in FY2025 from 16.7% in FY2024, so the moat is widening operationally but not yet translating cleanly into below-the-line progression every year ([Bajaj Auto finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJAJ-AUTO.NS)).
- Debate: The argument against it is that a lot of quality is already in the price, while exports, competition, and EV transition can all make the earnings path lumpier than it first appears ([Bajaj Auto finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJAJ-AUTO.NS)).

## CAMS

- Pitch: CAMS is effectively a toll-road on Indian financialization, because it sits in the plumbing of mutual-fund and adjacent asset-servicing flows without taking direct balance-sheet risk ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)).
- Evidence: This is one of the clearest moat stories in the portfolio because the advantage is infrastructure position, not product fashion: once you sit in the recordkeeping and processing layer of fund flows, incumbency and trust matter a lot ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)). Revenue rose from INR 9.7 billion in FY2023 to INR 14.2 billion in FY2025, net income increased from INR 2.9 billion to INR 4.7 billion, operating margin expanded from 61.2% to 65.2%, and net margin expanded from 29.4% to 33.1%, so this is one of the few names where it is fair to say the moat is not just intact but widening ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)).
- Debate: The main concern is that this is an obviously good business and therefore rarely cheap, so the stock depends on maintaining its quasi-infrastructure status in investor minds ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)).

## Rallis India

- Pitch: Rallis is more of a recovery and execution name than a pristine compounder, with the case built around crop-protection normalization, seeds, and operational improvement in agri-inputs ([Rallis India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/RALLIS.NS)).
- Evidence: The competitive advantage here is much thinner than for the stronger holdings because crop-input businesses are more exposed to pricing, demand, and channel swings, so you need to underwrite recovery rather than moat expansion ([Rallis India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/RALLIS.NS)). Revenue slipped from INR 29.7 billion in FY2023 to INR 26.6 billion in FY2025, operating margin moved from 5.9% to 6.2% after a FY2024 bounce, and net margin recovered only to 4.7%, which says the franchise is under pressure rather than visibly strengthening; this is a turnaround-underwriting idea, not a widening-moat compounder ([Rallis India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/RALLIS.NS)).
- Debate: That also explains the risk, because if execution and demand do not improve meaningfully, the stock can look expensive for what is still a relatively modest earnings base ([Rallis India finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/RALLIS.NS)).

## Mahindra & Mahindra

- Pitch: M&M is one of the best ways to play Indian domestic cyclicals through two unusually strong franchises at once, namely SUVs and tractors, backed by better capital allocation than the group used to get credit for ([Mahindra & Mahindra finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/M%26M.NS)).
- Evidence: The real advantage is the combination of two strong domestic franchises, utility vehicles and farm equipment, plus much-improved capital allocation discipline compared with the old conglomerate discount era ([Mahindra & Mahindra finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/M%26M.NS)). Revenue rose from INR 1.19 trillion in FY2023 to INR 1.56 trillion in FY2025, operating profit increased from INR 159.9 billion to INR 245.8 billion, and operating margin improved from 13.4% to 15.8%, which suggests the core businesses are higher quality than many investors used to assume; net margin, however, stayed broadly flat around 8.3%, so the moat looks stronger and the business mix cleaner, but not yet like a business with obvious structural margin expansion still ahead ([Mahindra & Mahindra finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/M%26M.NS)).
- Debate: The risk is that you are still buying a cyclical manufacturer, so the stock needs sustained strength in farm and utility-vehicle demand to keep compounding ([Mahindra & Mahindra finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/M%26M.NS)).

## Escorts Kubota

- Pitch: Escorts Kubota is a focused farm-mechanization and construction-equipment play where the core attraction is improving product quality, distribution leverage, and strategic support from Kubota ([Escorts Kubota finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ESCORTS.NS)).
- Evidence: Its advantage is specialist positioning in farm equipment with improving product quality and channel reach, and the Kubota relationship should raise standards over time rather than just add branding ([Escorts Kubota finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ESCORTS.NS)). Revenue rose from INR 84.3 billion in FY2023 to INR 102.4 billion in FY2025, net income nearly doubled to INR 12.6 billion, and net margin improved from 7.6% to 12.3%, although operating margin slipped slightly from FY2024 to 18.4% in FY2025, so the moat looks stable to modestly improving but not yet clearly widening in a straight line ([Escorts Kubota finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ESCORTS.NS)).
- Debate: The challenge is that the story is still tied to agri and construction sentiment, so it can look very cyclical when demand expectations soften ([Escorts Kubota finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ESCORTS.NS)).

## Tata Motors CV

- Pitch: TMCV is the direct India commercial-vehicles and freight-cycle holding, so the case is really about infrastructure, replacement demand, construction activity, and Tata’s ability to capture those flows with a broad truck-and-bus portfolio ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/TMCV:IN)) ([NSE India](https://www.nseindia.com/get-quotes/equity?symbol=TMCV)).
- Evidence: The real edge in Tata CV is breadth of product, service reach, and installed-base economics in a market where uptime and financing support matter as much as sticker price, which is why management can still talk about defending share through customer value proposition even in a softer demand quarter ([Tata Motors Investors](https://www.tatamotors.com/investors/)) ([Tata Motors Q1 FY26 results](https://www.tatamotors.com/press-releases/tata-motors-consolidated-q1-fy26-results/)). The freshest hard evidence is that Q1 FY26 CV revenue was INR 17,009 crore, EBITDA margin improved to 12.2%, EBIT margin to 9.7%, ROCE reached 39.6%, and domestic CV VAHAN share was 36.1% despite subdued industry demand, which suggests the moat is cyclical but still holding and perhaps widening modestly through realizations and cost savings rather than through outright volume growth ([Tata Motors Q1 FY26 results](https://www.tatamotors.com/press-releases/tata-motors-consolidated-q1-fy26-results/)) ([Tata Motors Investors](https://www.tatamotors.com/investors/)).
- Debate: This remains a cyclical industrial vehicle name, so if replacement demand, infrastructure spending, or pricing discipline soften, the market will punish it quickly ([Tata Motors Q1 FY26 results](https://www.tatamotors.com/press-releases/tata-motors-consolidated-q1-fy26-results/)).

## Bajaj Finance

- Pitch: Bajaj Finance is still one of the clearest plays on Indian consumer and SME credit compounding, with product breadth, underwriting data, and distribution scale that smaller NBFCs struggle to match ([Bajaj Finance finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJFINANCE.NS)).
- Evidence: Bajaj Finance’s edge is the combination of data, product breadth, and distribution velocity, which means it can keep originating across multiple retail and SME niches while controlling risk better than most subscale lenders ([Bajaj Finance finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJFINANCE.NS)). Revenue increased from INR 395.3 billion in FY2023 to INR 671.3 billion in FY2025 and net income rose from INR 115.1 billion to INR 166.4 billion, but the margin profile has been slipping as the book normalizes, with operating margin down from 39.3% in FY2023 to 32.9% in FY2025 and net margin down from 29.1% to 24.8%, so the moat still looks intact but it does not look like a widening-moat phase at the moment ([Bajaj Finance finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJFINANCE.NS)).
- Debate: The reason this is always debated is valuation, because when a lender already trades on a premium multiple, even small disappointments in growth, funding, or credit costs matter ([Bajaj Finance finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BAJFINANCE.NS)).

## Closing take

The portfolio’s Indian book still looks deliberately tilted toward high-quality financials, formalization beneficiaries, and a handful of cyclical names where the argument is “better business than the cycle implies” rather than “deep value” ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)) ([ICICI Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIBANK.NS)) ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)) ([Mahindra & Mahindra finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/M%26M.NS)).

If you want the shortest way to sound sharper in the meeting, it is to classify the names into three buckets. The clear widening-moat cases are CAMS, ICICI Lombard, and probably Airtel. The stable high-quality franchises are Kotak, ICICI, HCLTech, Colgate, Maruti, and HDFC Bank, though HDFC is still digesting the merger. The more cyclical or recovery-underwriting names are Bosch, M&M, Escorts, Tata CV, Rallis, and to some extent Bajaj Auto and Bajaj Finance ([CAMS finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/CAMS.NS)) ([ICICI Lombard finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/ICICIGI.NS)) ([Bharti Airtel finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/BHARTIARTL.NS)) ([HDFC Bank finance](https://perplexity.ai/finance/HDFCBANK.NS)).
