# Raia Drogasil — The Seven Questions

Raia Drogasil is the thirtieth-largest position in the GEM portfolio at zero-point-nine percent, with a market cap of around ten billion US dollars. Here is Raia Drogasil in seven questions.

---

## Cold open

Raia Drogasil just closed 2025 with gross revenue of forty-seven-point-six billion Brazilian reais, up fourteen percent year-on-year, and fourth-quarter retail growth of twenty-two percent. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation reached three-point-four billion reais, up nearly thirteen percent. Three hundred and thirty new pharmacies opened in 2025, taking the network to three thousand five hundred and forty-seven units across six hundred and sixty-three cities. Digital revenue grew nearly sixty percent to eleven-point-three billion reais and now represents twenty-nine percent of retail revenue. Return on invested capital reached seventeen percent. The company sold its specialty pharmacy 4Bio for seven hundred million reais at an estimated seventeen-percent internal rate of return. Brazilian pharmacy market share rose to nineteen-and-a-half percent nationwide, with São Paulo at thirty-four percent.

This is the dominant Brazilian pharmacy compounder firing on store rollout, digital, and the GLP-1 wave at the same time.

Seven questions.

---

## Question One: What is it?

Raia Drogasil is the largest pharmacy chain in Brazil, formed by the 2011 merger of Drogasil and Droga Raia. The business operates through the Raia and Drogasil banners across thirty Brazilian states, with a presence in over six hundred cities, more than three thousand five hundred stores, and over fifty-one million customers served annually.

The retail core grew gross revenue fifteen-and-a-half percent for the year, twenty-two percent in the fourth quarter alone. Mature stores grew fourteen-and-a-half percent — well above Brazilian inflation. Branded prescription drugs grew twenty-one percent, generics nearly nineteen percent, over-the-counter eleven percent, and health-and-personal-care eleven percent. Each category is gaining share within the Brazilian pharmacy ecosystem.

The digital business is now a real second leg. Digital revenue reached eleven-point-three billion reais, up fifty-nine percent. Twenty-nine percent of retail revenue now flows through digital channels, with eighty-two percent of digital sales through the company's mobile app. Ninety-seven percent of digital orders are fulfilled within sixty minutes. This is best-in-class Brazilian e-commerce execution applied to pharmacy.

The thing to internalise: Raia Drogasil is the consolidating winner of the Brazilian pharmacy industry, with a network density advantage, a proven digital platform, and disproportionate share in São Paulo — Brazil's largest market.

## Question Two: Can we trust them?

Yes — and the post-2024 management transition plus the disciplined 4Bio divestment is the evidence.

A new management cycle began in January 2025, with renewed focus on execution, efficiency, and core retail. The team delivered an accelerated growth trajectory and improved margins through the second half of 2025.

The past-stress case that matters is the 4Bio decision. The specialty pharmacy business — historically a strategic optionality leg — was sold for seven hundred million reais at a seventeen-percent internal rate of return on the original investment, with a roughly ten-times return overall. Most management teams keep optionality businesses for too long. This management team monetised when the price was right and explicitly redirected focus to core pharmacy retail and digital. The capital discipline signal is real.

The fourth-quarter print confirms the signals: earnings margin expansion from six-point-two to seven-point-two percent in twelve months. Return on invested capital at seventeen-point-one percent, up forty basis points. Cash cycle improved by nearly three days. Selling expenses contained at seventeen-point-five percent of gross revenue. Dividend and interest-on-equity distributions of seven hundred and seventy million reais. Net debt to earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation at one-point-two times. Customer satisfaction scores reached record levels.

The watch item is GLP-1 supply discipline. The category is a major growth driver but supply constraints and future generic pricing pressure could compress margin if not managed carefully.

## Question Three: Why does value compound?

Three engines, plus a kicker.

The first engine is store rollout into a consolidating market. Three hundred and thirty new pharmacies opened in 2025 with three hundred and thirty to three hundred and fifty more planned for 2026. The Brazilian pharmacy industry saw a net decrease in total pharmacies during 2025 — meaning Raia Drogasil is gaining stores in absolute terms while the broader market shrinks. Independent pharmacies are losing ground.

The second engine is the digital flywheel. Sixty-percent digital revenue growth is exceptional. The eighty-two-percent app penetration is structural — once customers download and use the app, repeat behaviour is high. Retail digital penetration at twenty-nine percent has runway toward the forty-plus percent that the best global pharmacy compounders achieve.

The third engine is GLP-1 leadership. Branded prescription drug growth at twenty-one percent for the year is partly the GLP-1 effect — Brazilian medical demand for weight-loss and diabetes management has scaled rapidly. The Brazilian GLP-1 market is projected to reach fifty billion reais. Raia Drogasil is the dominant prescription-pharmacy distribution channel for it.

The kicker is São Paulo market-share concentration. The state of São Paulo represents thirty-four-point-two percent market share, up three percentage points year-on-year. Disproportionate share in Brazil's most affluent state means above-average revenue per store and disproportionate profit contribution.

## Question Four: What proves us right, and what to track?

Six metrics. First, store count and rollout pace — three hundred and thirty new in 2025, three hundred and thirty to three hundred and fifty planned for 2026. Second, mature-store sales growth — fourteen-and-a-half percent today, well above Brazilian inflation. Third, digital revenue penetration — twenty-nine percent today, watch progression toward forty. Fourth, return on invested capital — seventeen percent; sustained progression confirms compounding. Fifth, market share — nineteen-and-a-half percent nationwide, with São Paulo at thirty-four. Sixth, GLP-1 segment performance — branded prescription growth, supply discipline, margin protection.

## Question Five: What's it worth?

Full-year 2025 adjusted net income was one-point-three-four billion reais, roughly two hundred and seventy million US dollars. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation was three-point-four billion reais. Free cash flow was one hundred and ninety-eight million. Market cap is around ten billion US dollars.

On trading multiples, Raia Drogasil trades at roughly thirty-five to forty times trailing earnings on the depressed near-term cash conversion, and around twelve to thirteen times trailing earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation. For a fifteen-percent-revenue-growth pharmacy compounder with seventeen-percent return on invested capital and digital scaling at sixty percent, that is reasonable but not cheap.

The simple framing: a Brazilian pharmacy compounder at the consolidating winner's premium, with structural digital and GLP-1 tailwinds. Comparable set: Walgreens Boots Alliance trades at much lower multiples for lower returns and slower growth; CVS Health similar; specialty pharmacy compounders globally at higher multiples. The Brazilian discount is the lever for re-rating if execution holds.

The key sensitivity is GLP-1 market evolution. A faster generic transition or supply-side normalisation compresses the high-growth wave. Second sensitivity is Brazilian consumer health and inflation dynamics.

## Question Six: Where is the market wrong?

Two angles. First: consensus prices the recent fourth-quarter cash consumption as a structural concern, when management has explicitly tied it to working-capital pressure from sales acceleration — a high-quality problem. As cash cycle continues improving, free cash flow normalises. Second: the 4Bio sale demonstrates that this management team is exceptional at monetising non-core assets at attractive returns. As the pharmacy retail business compounds, future portfolio decisions can crystallise additional value.

The honest counter: Brazilian retail multiples carry persistent macroeconomic and currency risk. Brazilian rates remain elevated, compressing the multiple. Position sizing should reflect the ongoing macro overhang.

## Question Seven: What could kill it, and when do we sell?

Real risks: GLP-1 supply or pricing shock; Brazilian consumer recession; competitive escalation from Pague Menos or Drogarias Brasil; digital execution missteps; macroeconomic and currency volatility; succession beyond the new management cycle.

Sell triggers: same-store sales growth decelerating below five percent for two consecutive quarters; return on invested capital falling below thirteen percent; digital revenue penetration stalling below thirty percent for two consecutive years; net debt to earnings rising above two times without strategic justification; GLP-1 segment producing material margin compression; current management team departing without credible succession.
